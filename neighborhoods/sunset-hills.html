The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Sunset Hills sits in a different conversation than most South County communities. Buyers usually come here looking for more land, more privacy, or a more premium feel than Concord, Mehlville, or Affton can deliver. The price points generally reflect that.

The Census Bureau's median owner-occupied value is above $523,000, which immediately puts Sunset Hills in a different band from the rest of this South County group. The lower density also helps explain why the city feels more open and less compressed than many nearby communities.
