Quick answers

Sunset Hills Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Short answers first. Each one links to the sales behind it further down the page.

How much do houses cost in Sunset Hills, Missouri?

In 30 sales inside Sunset Hills city limits that closed between January 14 and September 25, 2026, the median sale price was $729,050. The middle half sold between $526,250 and $958,750, and the full range ran from $162,500 to $2,150,000.

That’s a different measure from the Census median owner value for Sunset Hills (about $523,800), which reflects owners’ estimates of all homes, not recent sales.

Can I buy a house in Sunset Hills for $500,000?

7 of 30 sales (23.3%) closed at $500,000 or less. Of those 7, 5 had three or more bedrooms, 5 had two or more baths and 4 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,260 sq ft, built in 1956.

What does $800,000 buy in Sunset Hills?

19 of 30 sales (63.3%) closed at $800,000 or less. Of those 19, 17 had three or more bedrooms, 17 had two or more baths and 10 were ranch-style. Their median was 2,244 sq ft, built in 1957.

How fast do houses sell in Sunset Hills?

The median sale spent 4.5 days on market (CDOM). A quarter of the 30 sales took 3 days or less; the slowest quarter took about 12 days or more. 5 sales took 30+ days, 2 took 60+ and the longest took 70.

Do Sunset Hills homes sell over asking?

Measured against the original list price, 18 of 30 (60.0%) closed above it, 2 (6.7%) at it and 10 (33.3%) below it. The median sale was 104.3% of the original ask. From $700,000 to $899,999, 7 of 8 closed above the original ask.

What kind of houses are common in Sunset Hills?

A wider mix of eras than most of South County. Of the 30 sales, 14 (46.7%) were built before 1960, 5 (16.7%) from 1960 to 1979, 6 (20.0%) from 1980 to 1999 and 5 (16.7%) in 2000 or later. The median house was about 3,018 sq ft, built in 1969, and 10 of 30 (33.3%) listed a ranch style.

Does a Sunset Hills address mean the house is inside city limits?

Not on its own. Of the 30 sales inside Sunset Hills city limits, 16 listed “St Louis” as the city in MLS and 14 listed “Sunset Hills.” They spanned four ZIP codes: 63127 (21), 63128 (6), 63126 (2) and 63122 (1). Check the St. Louis County GIS map for the exact address.

Methodology

How This Sunset Hills Analysis Was Built

This analysis uses 30 closed-sale records from the STL Home Journey 2026 St. Louis County sales dataset (January 14–September 25, 2026). A sale counts here when its listing’s map coordinates fall inside the City of Sunset Hills’ boundary, using the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2024 TIGER/Line place boundary. The same records feed the St. Louis County school district housing analysis.

  • Historical closed sales are not current inventory.
  • Days on market uses CDOM.
  • Sale ÷ original ask compares the close price with the original list price.
  • Ranch share counts any MLS style that includes “Ranch.”
  • Listing coordinates can be imprecise near the boundary. Verify jurisdiction and school assignment for the exact address.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Sunset Hills sits in a different conversation than most South County communities. Buyers usually come here looking for more land, more privacy, or a more premium feel than Concord, Mehlville, or Affton can deliver. The price points generally reflect that.

The Census Bureau's median owner-occupied value is above $523,000, which immediately puts Sunset Hills in a different band from the rest of this South County group. The lower density also helps explain why the city feels more open and less compressed than many nearby communities.

Home Rules

Sunset Hills Occupancy and Home Rules

Sunset Hills is an incorporated city in St. Louis County with its own municipal government. A Sunset Hills mailing address or ZIP code does not by itself confirm that a property is inside city limits. City rules below apply when the property is within Sunset Hills city limits. Check the St. Louis County GIS map or contact the City before relying on them.

Do I need an occupancy permit when I buy or rent a home in Sunset Hills?

Yes. Sunset Hills requires a residential occupancy permit when occupancy changes, including after a sale, and the seller commonly applies before closing. The same occupancy process applies when a rental’s occupancy changes. See the City’s occupancy and rental registry page for current instructions.

Do I need a permit for a fence, deck, shed, or pool?

Sunset Hills requires Municipal Zoning Approval first, then a St. Louis County building permit and review by the applicable fire district. The City’s Unified Development Ordinance regulates these projects:

  • Fence: Regulated, with project-specific standards.
  • Deck: Regulated.
  • Shed: Regulated.
  • Pool: Regulated.

Requirements can depend on the property and project details. Check with the City before work begins.

Are electrical, plumbing, or mechanical permits required?

St. Louis County permits, plus review by the applicable fire district, apply to this work after City zoning approval. Check the City’s building permits page for the project’s requirements.

Do Sunset Hills landlords need a rental license or registration?

Yes. Sunset Hills requires annual rental-property registration, and the City maintains an owner/responsible-party registry. An occupancy permit is also required when residential occupancy changes.

Who handles property maintenance and code concerns?

The City of Sunset Hills handles enforcement, with St. Louis County in contracted roles.

How much does the occupancy permit cost?

The occupancy inspection is $25, and the application is valid for up to 6 months.

Before you buy, rent, or start a project: verify that the property is inside Sunset Hills city limits by exact address, then confirm current requirements with the City of Sunset Hills and St. Louis County where they apply.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: City of Sunset Hills occupancy and rental registry page, building permits page, and Unified Development Ordinance. Requirements are address- and project-dependent.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Before They Commit

FAQ

Where is Sunset Hills?

Sunset Hills is an incorporated city in South St. Louis County along the southwest side of the metro.

FAQ

What is Sunset Hills known for?

Buyers especially associate Sunset Hills with a more premium feel, larger lots, and landmarks like Laumeier Sculpture Park.

FAQ

Why is Sunset Hills usually pricier than much of South County?

Mostly because the houses are bigger. The median 2026 sale inside city limits was about 3,018 sq ft with 4 bedrooms, and price per square foot was about $238.

FAQ

How does Sunset Hills compare with Crestwood?

In 2026 sales, Crestwood’s median was $365,000 for about 1,646 sq ft. Sunset Hills’ median was $729,050 for about 3,018 sq ft.

FAQ

Who is Sunset Hills a good fit for?

Buyers who want the southwest-county feel and can support the larger monthly number that usually comes with it.

FAQ

What is Laumeier Sculpture Park?

Laumeier Sculpture Park is a 105-acre outdoor sculpture museum on the edge of Sunset Hills — free to the public, managed by St. Louis County Parks, and one of the most distinctive cultural landmarks in South County.
