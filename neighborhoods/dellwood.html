Dellwood Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records. 21 closed sales inside Dellwood city limits.

How much do houses cost in Dellwood, Missouri?

In 21 sales inside Dellwood city limits that closed between January 15 and September 23, 2026, the median sale price was $126,000. The middle half sold between $95,000 and $143,500, and the full range ran from $51,000 to $165,900.

Can I buy a house in Dellwood for $150,000?

18 of 21 sales (85.7%) closed at $150,000 or less. Of those 18, 15 had three or more bedrooms and 12 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,014 sq ft, built in 1958.

How fast do homes sell in Dellwood?

The median was 54 days on market (CDOM), with the middle half between 17 and 114 days. 14 of the 21 sales took 30 days or more.

Do homes in Dellwood sell over asking price?

Some do, but most did not. Of the 21 sales, 6 closed above the original list price, 1 at it and 14 below it. The median sale was 95.8% of the original asking price.

How old are most homes in Dellwood?

The median house was built in 1959 and had 1,014 sq ft. By year built, the 21 sales broke down this way: 2 before 1950, 19 from 1950 to 1969, 0 from 1970 to 1999 and 0 in 2000 or later. The median home had 3 bedrooms.

How much does a 3-bedroom house cost in Dellwood?

In 2026, the 17 three-bedroom homes that sold inside Dellwood city limits had a median price of $127,000; the middle half sold between $115,000 and $143,500. The median home had 1,014 sq ft, built in 1959.

How much does a ranch house cost in Dellwood?

In 2026, the 15 ranch-style homes that sold inside Dellwood city limits had a median price of $127,000; the middle half sold between $116,000 and $146,750. The median home had 3 bedrooms and 1,014 sq ft, built in 1959.

How much does a house with 2 bathrooms cost in Dellwood?

In 2026, the 9 homes with two or more bathrooms that sold inside Dellwood city limits had a median price of $127,000; the middle half sold between $115,000 and $145,000. The median home had 3 bedrooms and 1,014 sq ft, built in 1958.

Which school district is Dellwood in?

More than one. Of the 21 sales inside Dellwood city limits, 11 were in the Riverview Gardens School District, 6 in the Ferguson-Florissant School District and 4 in the Hazelwood School District. District lines don’t always follow city limits, so verify the assigned schools for the exact address before you write an offer.

How much are property taxes in Dellwood?

It depends on which school district side of Dellwood the house is on. In 2026 sales inside Dellwood city limits, the median annual property tax was $2,254 on the Riverview Gardens side (about 1.91% of the sale price) and $2,125 on the Ferguson-Florissant side (about 2.09% of the sale price). The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so use these medians to compare areas, then confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue before you make an offer.

Schools

Schools in Dellwood, Missouri

Dellwood is served by the Ferguson-Florissant School District. School district assignment in this area affects both buyer demand and long-term resale. Buyers with school-age children — or with future resale in mind — should verify which district serves the specific property address before committing. District boundaries do not always follow city or neighborhood lines.

⚠ School boundaries can vary by exact property address. Verify assigned schools directly with the district, county records, or your buyer agent before writing an offer.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Dellwood works best for buyers who understand what it is. It is a North County municipality that tends to attract practical buyers focused on established housing and reachable pricing. That matters because people who shop this market usually are not buying a headline; they are buying fit, payment, and the way the location works Monday through Friday.

In North County, the strongest pages are the honest ones. Dellwood tends to reward buyers who want established housing and practical value instead of prestige branding. The right buy here is usually about condition, block, and commute pattern more than trend.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

Dellwood sits in inner north county, which means buyers usually evaluate it through the lens of access, block-to-block feel, and nearby service corridors. That is why comparing it only by city name misses the point. In this part of the metro, the micro-location often matters as much as the municipality itself.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

What buyers actually find in Dellwood is older single-family neighborhoods with a steady residential feel. The market is usually less about flash and more about whether the house solves the buyer’s real problem: payment, space, commute, or setting.

That is why Dellwood is usually best judged against Moline Acres, Jennings, Ferguson, not against the metro’s highest-status suburbs. Buyers who shop it on its own terms often find more house-for-the-money than they expected.
