Hillsboro Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records. 64 closed sales with a Hillsboro mailing address.

How much does a house cost in Hillsboro, Missouri?

The 2026 median sale price was $357,500 across 64 sales with a Hillsboro mailing address, according to MLS sales records. The middle half sold between $290,000 and $460,000.

What does $300,000 buy in Hillsboro?

20 of 64 sales (31.2%) closed at $300,000 or less. Of those, 16 had three or more bedrooms and 8 were ranch-style; the median was 1,565 sq ft, built in 1984.

How fast do homes sell in Hillsboro?

The median 2026 sale spent 41.5 days on market (CDOM), against 18.5 days countywide. 45 of 64 sales took 30 days or more.

Do Hillsboro homes sell over asking price?

14 of 64 sales in 2026 (21.9%) closed above the original list price, compared with 38.5% countywide.

What kind of houses sell in Hillsboro?

The median 2026 sale was about 1,916 sq ft, built in 2002, and 36 of 64 (56.2%) were ranch-style.

Is a Hillsboro address inside Hillsboro city limits?

Not always. Of the 64 sales in 2026 with a Hillsboro mailing address, 18 were inside Hillsboro city limits and 46 were outside, according to U.S. Census Bureau 2024 boundaries. Inside the city, the city’s rules apply.

Which school district is Hillsboro in?

More than one. The 64 sales with a Hillsboro mailing address in 2026 were in Hillsboro R-III (61), Grandview R-II (2) and Fox C-6 (1). Confirm the district for the exact address.

How much are property taxes in Hillsboro?

The median annual property tax on homes that sold with a Hillsboro mailing address in 2026 was $2,278, and the middle half of bills ran from $1,930 to $2,979, across 56 sales, according to MLS sales records. The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so confirm the exact address with the Jefferson County Collector of Revenue.

Deciding on a house in Hillsboro

Can I negotiate on a Hillsboro house?

More than in most of the county. Hillsboro’s 41.5-day median was the slowest of the larger communities in 2026, and 45 of 64 sales sat 30 days or more. Those closed at a median 95.6% of the original asking price. With that much time on your side, keep the contingencies that protect your earnest money.

Is it cheaper to live inside Hillsboro city limits?

The houses are, the taxes aren’t. The 18 sales inside the city had a $294,470 median for about 1,320 sq ft, built around 2010; the 46 Hillsboro-address sales outside had a $413,000 median for about 2,532 sq ft. Median tax bills were nearly the same ($2,289 inside, $2,278 outside), so you pay similar taxes on a much cheaper house inside the city.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Hillsboro works best for buyers who understand what it is. It is the county seat and one of Jefferson County's most established civic centers, with a stronger town-center identity than many surrounding areas. That matters because people who shop this market usually are not buying a headline; they are buying fit, payment, and the way the location works Monday through Friday.

In Jefferson County, buyers are usually balancing distance, land, monthly cost, and pace. Hillsboro fits when the buyer wants some separation from tighter county-line suburbs but still needs a location that feels workable, not remote.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

Hillsboro sits in central jefferson county, and that location changes the lifestyle equation immediately. In Jefferson County, highway path, topography, and day-to-day errand distance all matter. Buyers who match the map to their real routine usually make better decisions here.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

What buyers actually find in Hillsboro is a mix of traditional neighborhood homes, newer subdivisions, and edge-of-town parcels. The market is usually less about flash and more about whether the house solves the buyer’s real problem: payment, space, commute, or setting.

That is why Hillsboro is usually best judged against De Soto, Festus, House Springs, not against inner-ring county suburbs with a totally different land pattern and pricing ladder. The right Jefferson County buy is often the one that gives you the most usable life for the payment.
