Maryland Heights, Missouri.

A $300,000 median and 7 days on market in 2026, split between the Pattonville and Parkway school districts, at the corner where North County meets West County. I’ve listed and sold homes here, so this page puts the 2026 numbers next to what I saw on my own sales.

Quick answers

Maryland Heights Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records. 72 closed single-family sales inside Maryland Heights city limits.

How much do houses cost in Maryland Heights, Missouri?

In 72 sales inside Maryland Heights city limits that closed between January 6 and September 29, 2026, the median sale price was $300,000. The middle half sold between $265,000 and $335,500, and the full range ran from $151,000 to $740,000.

Is Maryland Heights North County or West County?

Both labels get used, because the city sits where they meet. FOX 2 St. Louis calls the regions informal and notes that the north–south stretch of I-270 runs through Maryland Heights while the city also touches I-70, the usual line for North County. I group it with North County, and most of its 2026 sales were in the Pattonville School District (50 of 72), which also serves Bridgeton and St. Ann.

Can I buy a house in Maryland Heights for $300,000?

Yes. 37 of 72 sales (51.4%) closed at $300,000 or less. All 37 had three or more bedrooms, 28 had two or more baths and 25 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,400 sq ft, built in 1965.

What does $350,000 buy in Maryland Heights?

Most of the market. 57 of 72 sales (79.2%) closed at $350,000 or less, with a median of 1,505 sq ft built in 1967; 48 of them had two or more baths.

How fast do homes sell in Maryland Heights?

Fast. The median was 7 days on market (CDOM), with the middle half between 3 and 29 days. 18 of the 72 sales took 30 days or more.

Do homes in Maryland Heights sell over asking price?

About half do. Of the 72 sales, 37 closed above the original list price, 5 at it and 30 below it. The median sale was 100.0% of the original asking price.

How old are most homes in Maryland Heights?

The median sale was built in 1968, and 61 of the 72 sales were built before 1980. The median home had 1,592 square feet of living area.

How much does a 3-bedroom house cost in Maryland Heights?

The 53 three-bedroom sales had a median price of $285,000. The 37 ranch-style sales had a median of $274,000.

Which school district is Maryland Heights in?

Two. Of the 72 sales in 2026, 50 were in the Pattonville School District and 22 in the Parkway School District. The city name doesn’t settle the district, so verify it for the exact address before you write an offer.

Is a Maryland Heights house more expensive on the Parkway side?

In 2026, yes. The 22 Parkway-side sales had a $333,500 median against $280,050 for the 50 Pattonville-side sales, though the typical house was about the same size (1,566 vs. 1,602 sq ft) and age (both built around 1968). Parkway-side homes also took longer to sell: 11.5 days on median against 5.5.

How much are property taxes in Maryland Heights?

It depends on the school district side. In 2026 sales inside city limits, the median annual property tax was $3,280 on the Pattonville side (about 1.18% of the sale price) and $3,532 on the Parkway side (about 1.06%), according to MLS sales records. The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue.

Do I need an occupancy inspection in Maryland Heights?

Yes. The City of Maryland Heights requires its own occupancy inspection every time ownership transfers. We had one done on both of my 2026 sales here, and the city can be behind. If you’re selling, schedule the occupancy inspection as soon as you decide to sell, because it can take a few weeks in some cases. Get the current process and fee from the city. Buyers: it’s the city’s check, not a substitute for yours, and limiting your own inspection won’t change it.
