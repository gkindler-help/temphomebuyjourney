FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About South County’s Small Towns

Which school districts serve these small South County towns?

Several. Of the 38 2026 sales across these seven towns, 12 were in the Lindbergh School District, 9 were in the Affton School District, 9 were in the Webster Groves School District, 6 were in the Bayless School District and 2 were in the Rockwood School District. The town name doesn’t settle the district, so verify it for the exact address.

Are these towns incorporated?

Yes. The Census Bureau lists each one as an incorporated city, village or town, so each sets its own local rules. Each town’s section below has its occupancy, sewer-lateral and permit rules, last verified September 25, 2026.

Why put these towns on one page?

Most of them had only one to three sales in 2026, too few for a meaningful page of their own. Fenton and Shrewsbury had more and get fuller sections here.
