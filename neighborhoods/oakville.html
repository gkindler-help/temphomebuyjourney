Schools

Schools in Oakville

Oakville is served by the Mehlville School District. It is the highest-demand pocket inside that district, and it shows up in the prices. Buyers shopping Oakville are often paying a meaningful premium over nearby Mehlville or Lemay streets specifically because of school perception and resale confidence. If schools are your driver, Oakville is where most family buyers inside Mehlville want to land — which means stronger competition and faster-moving inventory when a clean home is priced right.

⚠ School boundaries can vary by exact property address. Verify assigned schools directly with the district, county records, or your buyer agent before writing an offer.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Oakville is one of the clearer long-term ownership markets in South County. It is physically larger than many nearby communities, and it usually attracts buyers who are shopping for staying power: more house, more suburban spread, and a neighborhood feel that leans stable instead of transitional.

The Census Bureau puts Oakville's owner-occupied rate at 86%, almost identical to Concord's, but Oakville sits on a much larger footprint. That larger scale changes the way buyers search it: not as a single pocket, but as a broad South County market with distinct subareas and lot patterns.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

Mehlville School District says it serves Oakville, and county parks help define the geography. Cliff Cave Park offers river bluff and Mississippi Greenway trail access, while Bee Tree Park adds trails, a mansion, and one of the more distinctive natural settings in South County. Those anchors reinforce Oakville's bigger, more edge-of-county feel.

Questions Buyers Ask Before They Commit

FAQ

Where is Oakville?

Oakville is an unincorporated South St. Louis County community bordering the river side of the county.

FAQ

What is Oakville known for?

Oakville is known for larger suburban geography, strong owner occupancy, and access to parks like Cliff Cave and Bee Tree.

FAQ

Why do buyers search Oakville so often?

Because it offers a broad inventory field and a strong long-term ownership profile within South County.

FAQ

How does Oakville compare with Concord?

Oakville is much larger geographically and can feel more spread out, while Concord often feels tighter and more central to the retail core.

FAQ

Who is Oakville a good fit for?

Buyers who want suburban scale, stability, and room to search within one broad South County market.

FAQ

What school district serves Oakville?

Oakville is served by the Mehlville School District. Verify exact school assignment by address — Oakville is large enough that individual block assignments can vary.

FAQ

What is the median home price in Oakville?

Census data puts the median owner-occupied home value in Oakville around $318,600 — the highest in the Mehlville district footprint.

FAQ

What parks are near Oakville?

Cliff Cave County Park and Bee Tree County Park are the two signature parks on Oakville's river edge. Cliff Cave offers Mississippi Greenway trail access; Bee Tree includes trails, a historic mansion, and one of the more distinctive natural settings in South County.

Parks & Lifestyle

Bee Tree Park — What Oakville Families Actually Use

Bee Tree Park is one of the reasons families target Oakville specifically. 281 acres of Saint Louis County parkland on the Mississippi bluffs — wooded trails, picnic areas, and the kind of outdoor access that is hard to find this close to the county's residential core.
