Schools

Schools in Oakville

Oakville is served by the Mehlville School District. It is the highest-demand pocket inside that district, and it shows up in the prices. Buyers shopping Oakville are often paying a meaningful premium over nearby Mehlville or Lemay streets specifically because of school perception and resale confidence. If schools are your driver, Oakville is where most family buyers inside Mehlville want to land — which means stronger competition and faster-moving inventory when a clean home is priced right.

⚠ School boundaries can vary by exact property address. Verify assigned schools directly with the district, county records, or your buyer agent before writing an offer.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Oakville is one of the clearer long-term ownership markets in South County. It is physically larger than many nearby communities, and it usually attracts buyers who are shopping for staying power: more house, more suburban spread, and a neighborhood feel that leans stable instead of transitional.

The Census Bureau puts Oakville's owner-occupied rate at 86%, almost identical to Concord's, but Oakville sits on a much larger footprint. That larger scale changes the way buyers search it: not as a single pocket, but as a broad South County market with distinct subareas and lot patterns.

Occupancy

Oakville Occupancy Rules for Buyers, Sellers & Landlords

Oakville is a community name, not a municipality. If the property sits in unincorporated St. Louis County, the County handles occupancy, permits and code enforcement there; there is no Oakville city hall.

A re-occupancy permit is required when a new occupant moves in, after a sale or a rental change. The County occupancy application recognizes both SALE and RENTAL, and the home can’t be occupied until the permit is issued.

The current County occupancy inspection fee isn’t published on the downloadable application. The fee must accompany the application, so confirm the amount in the County permitting portal or current fee schedule.

Buyers: build the County occupancy inspection into the closing timeline. Sellers: apply early enough that the permit doesn’t hold up closing.

Source: St. Louis County Transportation & Public Works — Single Family, Two-Family and Townhouse Occupancy Permit — Unincorporated St. Louis County · Verified September 25, 2026

Sewer Lateral

Sewer Lateral Coverage in Oakville

Applies only if the exact address is in unincorporated St. Louis County.

If it is, the home may be covered by St. Louis County’s Sewer Lateral Repair Program, subject to eligibility. The program covers a defective residential sewer lateral from the foundation to the sewer main, with stated exclusions.

Read the eligibility rules and exclusions before you count on coverage, especially if an inspection or sewer scope turns up a lateral problem.

Source: St. Louis County Planning — Ordinance Guidebook — Sewer Lateral Repair Program · Verified September 25, 2026

Permits

Permits for Home Projects in Oakville

Applies only if the exact address is in unincorporated St. Louis County.

For unincorporated addresses, St. Louis County Transportation & Public Works issues applicable residential permits.

  • Fence: a fence 6 ft or less generally doesn’t need a building permit, but zoning, sight-distance and private subdivision restrictions still apply. (Ordinance Guidebook)
  • Shed or accessory structure: a shed over 120 sq ft requires a building permit. (Ordinance Guidebook)
  • Pool: qualifying pools need a County permit plus enclosure, setback and electrical compliance. (Ordinance Guidebook)
  • Deck: County permit and code review as applicable. (Permits & Applications)
  • Electrical, plumbing & mechanical: St. Louis County trade permits as applicable. (Permits & Applications)

Source: St. Louis County Transportation & Public Works — Permits & Applications · Verified September 25, 2026

Rentals & Code

Renting It Out & Code Enforcement in Oakville

Applies only if the exact address is in unincorporated St. Louis County.

Renting it out: the County occupancy process applies to rentals, so each tenant change triggers a re-occupancy permit. Separate landlord rules may also apply; confirm them before you list. (Single Family, Two-Family and Townhouse Occupancy Permit — Unincorporated St. Louis County)

Property maintenance: code issues on unincorporated property go to St. Louis County Neighborhood Preservation. (Ordinance Guidebook)

Sources: St. Louis County Transportation & Public Works — Single Family, Two-Family and Townhouse Occupancy Permit — Unincorporated St. Louis County · St. Louis County Planning — Ordinance Guidebook · Verified September 25, 2026

FAQ

Where is Oakville?

Oakville is an unincorporated South St. Louis County community bordering the river side of the county.

FAQ

What is Oakville known for?

Oakville is known for larger suburban geography, strong owner occupancy, and access to parks like Cliff Cave and Bee Tree.

FAQ

Why do buyers search Oakville so often?

Because it offers a broad inventory field and a strong long-term ownership profile within South County.

FAQ

How does Oakville compare with Concord?

Oakville is much larger geographically and can feel more spread out, while Concord often feels tighter and more central to the retail core.

FAQ

Who is Oakville a good fit for?

Buyers who want suburban scale, stability, and room to search within one broad South County market.

FAQ

What school district serves Oakville?

Oakville is served by the Mehlville School District. Verify exact school assignment by address — Oakville is large enough that individual block assignments can vary.

FAQ

What is the median home price in Oakville?

Census data puts the median owner-occupied home value in Oakville around $318,600 — the highest in the Mehlville district footprint.

FAQ

What parks are near Oakville?

Cliff Cave County Park and Bee Tree County Park are the two signature parks on Oakville's river edge. Cliff Cave offers Mississippi Greenway trail access; Bee Tree includes trails, a historic mansion, and one of the more distinctive natural settings in South County.
