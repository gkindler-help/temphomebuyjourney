Schools

Schools in Lemay

Lemay falls inside the Mehlville School District, but it is one of the more affordable entry points within that district. Buyers who specifically want the Mehlville district name and are working with a tighter budget often end up looking here, alongside Concord and parts of the Mehlville core. The tradeoff versus Oakville is real: same district, meaningfully different price and inventory feel. If the district access matters but Oakville pricing does not work, Lemay is worth a serious look.

⚠ School boundaries can vary by exact property address. Verify assigned schools directly with the district, county records, or your buyer agent before writing an offer.

The Honest Take

Practical

Lemay is one of the clearer examples of a market where buyers can still find South County access without taking on the monthly burden of higher-profile suburbs. The tradeoff is obvious: the housing stock is older, the branding is less polished, and the feel is more utilitarian than aspirational.

That does not make Lemay a weak market. It makes it a market where buyers need to understand the housing and buy intentionally. The Census Bureau's median owner-occupied value is notably lower than in many neighboring South County communities, which is exactly why Lemay remains relevant for cost-conscious buyers.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

Jefferson Barracks Park gives Lemay a landmark that reaches well beyond the neighborhood itself. County materials note the park's museums, monuments, trails, disc golf, and event spaces, while park history traces the military post back to 1826. That institutional weight gives the area more identity than buyers sometimes expect.

Home Rules

Lemay Occupancy and Home Rules

Lemay is an unincorporated community in St. Louis County, not a city with its own municipal government. A Lemay mailing address does not by itself confirm that a property is unincorporated. County rules below apply when the property is within unincorporated St. Louis County. Check the St. Louis County GIS map or contact the County before relying on them.

Do I need an occupancy permit when I buy or rent a home in Lemay?

If the property is in unincorporated St. Louis County, the County requires an occupancy permit when a new occupant moves in after a sale or rental change. The County’s application covers both sale and rental situations, and the new occupant must wait until the permit is issued before occupying the home.

See the County’s single-family dwelling occupancy application and permits and applications for current instructions.

Is there a sewer lateral repair program in Lemay?

Unincorporated properties may be eligible for the St. Louis County Sewer Lateral Repair Program. Eligibility applies; assistance is not automatic. Check the County’s current program requirements and ordinance guidebook before budgeting for a repair.

Do I need a permit for a fence, deck, shed, or pool?

St. Louis County Transportation and Public Works handles applicable residential permits for unincorporated properties. The County’s ordinance guidebook describes these general rules:

  • Fence: A fence 6 feet or shorter generally does not require a building permit. Zoning, sight-distance, and private subdivision or property restrictions may still apply.
  • Deck: Permit and code review may apply, depending on the project. Confirm requirements for the specific address and work before construction.
  • Shed: A shed larger than 120 square feet requires a building permit.
  • Pool: Qualifying pools require a County permit and must meet applicable enclosure, setback, and electrical requirements.

Requirements can depend on the property and project details. Check with the County before work begins.

Are electrical, plumbing, or mechanical permits required?

County trade permits may apply to electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work. Check the County’s permits and applications for the project’s requirements.

Do Lemay landlords need a rental license or registration?

A County occupancy permit is required when a new occupant moves in after a rental change at an unincorporated property. The available County information reviewed for this guide does not establish a separate local landlord license or registration requirement. Confirm with the County whether any additional rules apply to the property or rental arrangement.

Who handles property maintenance and code concerns?

For unincorporated properties, St. Louis County Neighborhood Preservation handles applicable property maintenance and code matters. Confirm that the address is in unincorporated County territory before contacting a city department.

How much does the occupancy permit cost?

The County’s downloadable occupancy application does not publish the current inspection fee. The fee must accompany the application, so confirm the current amount in the County permitting portal or with the County before submitting.

Before you buy, rent, or start a project: verify the property’s jurisdiction by exact address, then confirm current requirements with St. Louis County. Rules can differ for a property inside an incorporated municipality.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: St. Louis County Transportation and Public Works occupancy and permit pages; St. Louis County Planning ordinance guidebook. Requirements are address- and project-dependent.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Before They Commit

FAQ

Where is Lemay?

Lemay is an unincorporated South St. Louis County community near the Mississippi side of the county.

FAQ

What is Lemay known for?

Lemay is especially associated with Jefferson Barracks Park and a lower-cost South County entry point.

FAQ

Why do buyers compare Lemay and Affton?

Because both can serve price-conscious buyers, but Lemay often comes in at a lower valuation level while Affton tends to have a different location feel.

FAQ

Is Lemay mostly luxury housing?

No. Lemay is generally a practical, value-oriented market rather than a premium suburb.

FAQ

Who is Lemay a good fit for?

Buyers who prioritize payment, access, and realistic expectations about older housing.

FAQ

What school district serves Lemay?

Lemay is served by the Mehlville School District. Verify exact boundaries by address before making any school-driven decisions.

FAQ

What is the median home price in Lemay?

Census data puts the median owner-occupied home value in Lemay around $152,200 — one of the lowest in South St. Louis County. Actual prices vary by block and condition.

FAQ

What is Jefferson Barracks Park?

Jefferson Barracks County Park is a major South County anchor in Lemay — a historic military post established in 1826, now featuring museums, trails, disc golf, event spaces, and Mississippi River access.
