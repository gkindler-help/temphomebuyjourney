Quick answers

Affton Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Short answers first. Each one links to the sales behind it further down the page.

How much do houses cost in Affton, Missouri?

In 53 sales inside the Census boundary of the Affton community (CDP) that closed between January 9 and September 4, 2026, the median sale price was $273,000. The middle half sold between $250,000 and $315,000, and the full range ran from $161,900 to $648,000.

That’s a different measure from the Census median owner value for Affton (about $212,400), which reflects owners’ estimates of all homes, not recent sales.

Can I buy a house in Affton for $250,000?

14 of 53 sales (26.4%) closed at $250,000 or less. Of those 14, 5 had three or more bedrooms, 4 had two or more baths and 7 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,021 sq ft, built in 1950.

What does $300,000 buy in Affton?

37 of 53 sales (69.8%) closed at $300,000 or less. Of those 37, 18 had three or more bedrooms, 21 had two or more baths and 23 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,200 sq ft, built in 1954.

See what each budget reached →

How fast do houses sell in Affton?

The median sale spent 5 days on market (CDOM). A quarter of the 53 sales took 4 days or less; the slowest quarter took about 19 days or more. 8 sales took 30+ days, 1 took 60+ and the longest took 359.

See market time by price band →

Do Affton homes sell over asking?

Measured against the original list price, 33 of 53 (62.3%) closed above it, 8 (15.1%) at it and 12 (22.6%) below it. The median sale was 103.2% of the original ask. All 6 sales from $300,000 to $349,999 closed above the original ask, a small group but a consistent one.

What kind of houses are common in Affton?

Mostly houses from the 1940s and 1950s. Of the 53 sales, 35 (66.0%) were built before 1960, 12 (22.6%) from 1960 to 1979, 4 (7.5%) from 1980 to 1999 and 2 (3.8%) in 2000 or later. The median house was about 1,440 sq ft, built in 1954, and 30 of 53 (56.6%) listed a ranch style.

Does an Affton mailing address mean the house is in Affton?

Not necessarily. All 53 sales inside the Affton boundary were in ZIP 63123, but 63123 also reaches beyond it. Only 19 of the 53 listed “Affton” as the city in MLS; 24 listed “St Louis” and 9 “Unincorporated.” Check the St. Louis County GIS map for the exact address.

Is all of Affton in the Affton School District?

No. Of the 53 sales inside the Affton boundary, 41 were in the Affton School District, 7 in Mehlville R-IX, 4 in Bayless and 1 in Lindbergh. The community name doesn’t settle the district. Verify the assigned schools for the exact address.

Is Affton incorporated?

No. Affton is an unincorporated community in St. Louis County with no mayor or city council of its own, so County rules generally apply, including the occupancy permit required when a new occupant moves in after a sale. A mailing address alone doesn’t confirm that a property is unincorporated.

Methodology

How This Affton Analysis Was Built

This analysis uses 53 closed-sale records from the STL Home Journey 2026 St. Louis County sales dataset (January 9–September 4, 2026). A sale counts here when its listing’s map coordinates fall inside the Affton census-designated place (CDP), using the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2024 TIGER/Line place boundary. The same records feed the St. Louis County school district housing analysis.

  • Historical closed sales are not current inventory.
  • Days on market uses CDOM.
  • Sale ÷ original ask compares the close price with the original list price.
  • Ranch share counts any MLS style that includes “Ranch.”
  • Listing coordinates can be imprecise near the boundary. Verify jurisdiction and school assignment for the exact address.

Location & Identity

The Affton-Lemay Chamber points buyers toward the same practical anchors locals use every week: Grant's Trail, Grant's Farm, neighborhood retail, and quick access into the larger South County service grid. This is the kind of location that works best for people who want errands to be easy rather than glamorous.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

Affton tends to appeal to first-time buyers, move-down buyers, and anyone trying to stay in South County without jumping into the price bracket of Crestwood, Sunset Hills, or Webster. The tradeoff is that the housing stock is more modest and the lots are generally tighter. The upside is that the numbers are often easier to live with.

Home Rules

Affton Occupancy and Home Rules

Affton is an unincorporated community in St. Louis County, not a city with its own municipal government. An Affton mailing address does not by itself confirm that a property is unincorporated. County rules below apply when the property is within unincorporated St. Louis County. Check the St. Louis County GIS map or contact the County before relying on them.

Do I need an occupancy permit when I buy or rent a home in Affton?

If the property is in unincorporated St. Louis County, the County requires an occupancy permit when a new occupant moves in after a sale or rental change. The County’s application covers both sale and rental situations, and the new occupant must wait until the permit is issued before occupying the home.

See the County’s single-family dwelling occupancy application and permits and applications for current instructions.

Is there a sewer lateral repair program in Affton?

Unincorporated properties may be eligible for the St. Louis County Sewer Lateral Repair Program. Eligibility applies; assistance is not automatic. Check the County’s current program requirements and ordinance guidebook before budgeting for a repair.

Do I need a permit for a fence, deck, shed, or pool?

St. Louis County Transportation and Public Works handles applicable residential permits for unincorporated properties. The County’s ordinance guidebook describes these general rules:

  • Fence: A fence 6 feet or shorter generally does not require a building permit. Zoning, sight-distance, and private subdivision or property restrictions may still apply.
  • Deck: Permit and code review may apply, depending on the project. Confirm requirements for the specific address and work before construction.
  • Shed: A shed larger than 120 square feet requires a building permit.
  • Pool: Qualifying pools require a County permit and must meet applicable enclosure, setback, and electrical requirements.

Requirements can depend on the property and project details. Check with the County before work begins.

Are electrical, plumbing, or mechanical permits required?

County trade permits may apply to electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work. Check the County’s permits and applications for the project’s requirements.

Do Affton landlords need a rental license or registration?

A County occupancy permit is required when a new occupant moves in after a rental change at an unincorporated property. The available County information reviewed for this guide does not establish a separate local landlord license or registration requirement. Confirm with the County whether any additional rules apply to the property or rental arrangement.

Who handles property maintenance and code concerns?

For unincorporated properties, St. Louis County Neighborhood Preservation handles applicable property maintenance and code matters. Confirm that the address is in unincorporated County territory before contacting a city department.

How much does the occupancy permit cost?

The County’s downloadable occupancy application does not publish the current inspection fee. The fee must accompany the application, so confirm the current amount in the County permitting portal or with the County before submitting.

Before you buy, rent, or start a project: verify the property’s jurisdiction by exact address, then confirm current requirements with St. Louis County. Rules can differ for a property inside an incorporated municipality.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: St. Louis County Transportation and Public Works occupancy and permit pages; St. Louis County Planning ordinance guidebook. Requirements are address- and project-dependent.
