St. John Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records. 18 closed sales inside St. John city limits.

How much do houses cost in St. John, Missouri?

In 18 sales inside St. John city limits that closed between January 16 and September 4, 2026, the median sale price was $157,500. The middle half sold between $111,750 and $196,250, and the full range ran from $25,000 to $257,200.

Can I buy a house in St. John for $200,000?

14 of 18 sales (77.8%) closed at $200,000 or less. Of those 14, 6 had three or more bedrooms and 6 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,045 sq ft, built in 1946.

How fast do homes sell in St. John?

The median was 39 days on market (CDOM), with the middle half between 21 and 50 days. 10 of the 18 sales took 30 days or more.

Do homes in St. John sell over asking price?

Some do, but most did not. Of the 18 sales, 3 closed above the original list price, 1 at it and 14 below it. The median sale was 95.3% of the original asking price.

How old are most homes in St. John?

The median house was built in 1946 and had 1,162 sq ft. By year built, the 18 sales broke down this way: 11 before 1950, 7 from 1950 to 1969, 0 from 1970 to 1999 and 0 in 2000 or later.

How much does a 2-bedroom house cost in St. John?

In 2026, the 9 two-bedroom homes that sold inside St. John city limits had a median price of $117,000; the middle half sold between $90,000 and $155,000. The median home had 910 sq ft, built in 1942.

How much does a 3-bedroom house cost in St. John?

In 2026, the 5 three-bedroom homes that sold inside St. John city limits had a median price of $170,000, with prices from $25,000 to $200,000. The median home had 1,102 sq ft, built in 1952.

How much does a ranch house cost in St. John?

In 2026, the 7 ranch-style homes that sold inside St. John city limits had a median price of $170,000, with prices from $145,000 to $215,000. The median home had 3 bedrooms and 1,304 sq ft, built in 1952.

How much does a bungalow cost in St. John?

In 2026, the 6 bungalows that sold inside St. John city limits had a median price of $100,000, with prices from $35,000 to $236,000. The median home had 2 bedrooms and 820 sq ft, built in 1942.

How much does a house with 2 bathrooms cost in St. John?

In 2026, the 9 homes with two or more bathrooms that sold inside St. John city limits had a median price of $200,000; the middle half sold between $145,000 and $225,000. The median home had 3 bedrooms and 1,464 sq ft, built in 1952.

Which school district is St. John in?

Of the 18 sales inside St. John city limits, 17 were in the Ritenour School District; 1 listed another district. District lines don’t always follow city limits, so verify the assigned schools for the exact address before you write an offer.

How much are property taxes in St. John?

In 2026, most sales inside St. John city limits were in the Ritenour School District, and their median annual property tax was $2,067, about 1.31% of the sale price. The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so use this median to compare areas, then confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue before you make an offer.

Schools

Schools in St John, Missouri

St John is served by the Ritenour School District. School district assignment in this area affects both buyer demand and long-term resale. Buyers with school-age children — or with future resale in mind — should verify which district serves the specific property address before committing. District boundaries do not always follow city or neighborhood lines.

⚠ School boundaries can vary by exact property address. Verify assigned schools directly with the district, county records, or your buyer agent before writing an offer.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

St. John works best for buyers who understand what it is. It is an inner North County municipality with strong access and a value-first search profile. That matters because people who shop this market usually are not buying a headline; they are buying fit, payment, and the way the location works Monday through Friday.

In North County, the strongest pages are the honest ones. St. John tends to reward buyers who want established housing and practical value instead of prestige branding. The right buy here is usually about condition, block, and commute pattern more than trend.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

St. John sits in inner north county, which means buyers usually evaluate it through the lens of access, block-to-block feel, and nearby service corridors. That is why comparing it only by city name misses the point. In this part of the metro, the micro-location often matters as much as the municipality itself.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

What buyers actually find in St. John is older modest homes in a practical, connected location. The market is usually less about flash and more about whether the house solves the buyer’s real problem: payment, space, commute, or setting.

That is why St. John is usually best judged against Pagedale, Wellston, Normandy, not against the metro’s highest-status suburbs. Buyers who shop it on its own terms often find more house-for-the-money than they expected.
