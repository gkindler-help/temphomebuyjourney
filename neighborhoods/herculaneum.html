Herculaneum, Missouri.

A small Mississippi River city in Jefferson County, between Pevely and Festus along I-55. In 2026, 19 single-family homes sold with a Herculaneum mailing address, at a $319,900 median.

How much does a house cost in Herculaneum, Missouri?
