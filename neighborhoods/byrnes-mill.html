Byrnes Mill, Missouri.

A small city on Route 30 in northern Jefferson County, next to House Springs. In 2026, 11 single-family homes sold with a Byrnes Mill mailing address, at a $484,050 median.

How much does a house cost in Byrnes Mill, Missouri?

The 2026 median sale price was $484,050 across 11 sales with a Byrnes Mill mailing address, according to MLS sales records. The middle half sold between $422,500 and $490,000.

Is a Byrnes Mill address inside Byrnes Mill city limits?

Not always. Of the 11 sales in 2026 with a Byrnes Mill mailing address, 10 were inside Byrnes Mill city limits and 1 was outside, according to U.S. Census Bureau 2024 boundaries. Inside the city, the city’s rules apply.

Do I need an occupancy inspection to sell a house in Byrnes Mill?

Yes. The City of Byrnes Mill requires an occupancy inspection before a home is sold. One of the 2026 sales with a Byrnes Mill address was outside city limits, where Jefferson County rules apply instead; the county has no resale inspection of its own.

What does the Byrnes Mill occupancy inspection mean for me as a buyer?

It’s the city’s code check at the sale, and it doesn’t replace your own inspection. Ask whether it’s been done and whether any required repairs are complete before closing. The reverse is true too: limiting your own inspection doesn’t skip the city’s.
