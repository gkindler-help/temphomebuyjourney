Schools

Schools in Forest Park SE / The Grove

Forest Park Southeast is served by St. Louis Public Schools (SLPS). The neighborhood shares the same SLPS territory as the CWE and The Hill. Buyers who want proximity to WashU or BJC often factor private school into their housing costs here.

⚠ Always verify assigned school by exact property address with the district before making school-driven decisions.

The Honest Take

The Grove Is the Identity, Manchester Is the Street

Forest Park Southeast is the neighborhood. The Grove is the corridor. They are not the same thing, but buyers outside St. Louis often use the names interchangeably.

Manchester Avenue through Forest Park Southeast is one of the most recognized entertainment streets in the city — bars, venues, and restaurants in pre-war brick buildings behind the gateway arch. The residential fabric behind Manchester is quieter: brick flats, 2-story homes, and the kind of urban housing stock that goes with proximity to WashU and BJC.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Before They Commit

FAQ

Where is Forest Park Southeast?

Forest Park Southeast is in St. Louis City, in the 63110 zip code, south of Forest Park and east of The Hill. The Grove entertainment district runs along Manchester Avenue through the neighborhood.

FAQ

What is The Grove?

The Grove is the entertainment district along Manchester Avenue in Forest Park Southeast — bars, restaurants, and live venues in a pre-war brick commercial corridor, one of the most recognized nightlife destinations in St. Louis.

FAQ

What is the housing stock like?

Forest Park Southeast has pre-war brick flats and 2-story homes on residential streets behind the Manchester Avenue corridor, with older multifamily and mixed-use buildings on the main streets.

FAQ

What school district serves Forest Park Southeast?

Forest Park Southeast is served by St. Louis Public Schools (SLPS). Always verify the assigned school by exact property address.

FAQ

Why do buyers look here?

Proximity to WashU Medical, BJC, Forest Park, and The Grove corridor makes this neighborhood appealing to medical and academic buyers who want an urban location with recognized amenity access.
