High Ridge Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records. 54 closed sales with a High Ridge mailing address.

How much does a house cost in High Ridge, Missouri?

The 2026 median sale price was $318,950 across 54 sales with a High Ridge mailing address, according to MLS sales records. The middle half sold between $251,250 and $425,000.

What does $250,000 buy in High Ridge?

14 of 54 sales (25.9%) closed at $250,000 or less. Of those, 11 had three or more bedrooms and 7 were ranch-style; the median was 1,331 sq ft, built in 1963.

How fast do homes sell in High Ridge?

The median 2026 sale spent 14 days on market (CDOM), against 18.5 days countywide. 19 of 54 sales took 30 days or more.

Do High Ridge homes sell over asking price?

What kind of houses sell in High Ridge?

The median 2026 sale was about 2,072 sq ft, built in 1988, and 27 of 54 (50.0%) were ranch-style.

Is High Ridge an incorporated city?

No. High Ridge is unincorporated Jefferson County, with no city government, according to the U.S. Census Bureau. County rules apply to homes with a High Ridge address.

How much are property taxes in High Ridge?

The median annual property tax on homes that sold with a High Ridge mailing address in 2026 was $2,376, and the middle half of bills ran from $1,388 to $3,840, across 54 sales, according to MLS sales records. What should I check on a High Ridge house before I buy?

Start with how it handles waste water. High Ridge is unincorporated, and some homes use private septic systems; on public sewer, get a sewer scope. 21 of 54 sales were built before 1980, so look hard at the roof and mechanicals too.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

High Ridge works best for buyers who understand what it is. It is one of northwest Jefferson County's major community names, attracting buyers who want a hillier, less compressed alternative to the county line suburbs. That matters because people who shop this market usually are not buying a headline; they are buying fit, payment, and the way the location works Monday through Friday.

In Jefferson County, buyers are usually balancing distance, land, monthly cost, and pace. High Ridge fits when the buyer wants some separation from tighter county-line suburbs but still needs a location that feels workable, not remote.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

High Ridge sits in northwest jefferson county, and that location changes the lifestyle equation immediately. In Jefferson County, highway path, topography, and day-to-day errand distance all matter. Buyers who match the map to their real routine usually make better decisions here.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

What buyers actually find in High Ridge is a broad mix of subdivisions, older homes, and lower-density pockets. The market is usually less about flash and more about whether the house solves the buyer’s real problem: payment, space, commute, or setting.

That is why High Ridge is usually best judged against House Springs, Cedar Hill, Fenton, not against inner-ring county suburbs with a totally different land pattern and pricing ladder. The right Jefferson County buy is often the one that gives you the most usable life for the payment.
