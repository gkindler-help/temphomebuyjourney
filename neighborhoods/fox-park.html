Schools in Fox Park

The Honest Take

Shenandoah Avenue and What Sits Behind It

Fox Park is defined by Shenandoah Avenue more than any other street. The pre-war mixed-use buildings that line that corridor — brick above retail, ornate cornices, Victorian bay details — tell you exactly what kind of neighborhood this is and has been.

Behind the Shenandoah corridor are tighter residential streets with 2-story brick flats and smaller homes. The neighborhood is compact. That density is the characteristic: Fox Park feels like a working South City neighborhood that kept its fabric rather than losing it.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Before They Commit

FAQ

Where is Fox Park?

Fox Park is in South St. Louis City, in the 63104 zip code, along and near Shenandoah Avenue between Jefferson and Grand.

FAQ

What is the housing stock like in Fox Park?

Fox Park has a mix of pre-war brick residential and mixed-use commercial buildings — 2 and 3-story brick with ground-floor commercial on Shenandoah Avenue, and tighter residential streets behind the main corridor.

FAQ

What school district serves Fox Park?

Fox Park is served by St. Louis Public Schools (SLPS). Always verify the assigned school by exact property address.

FAQ

How does Fox Park compare to Soulard?

Both are South City neighborhoods with older brick character. Soulard has a stronger nightlife and destination identity. Fox Park is quieter with more working-neighborhood feel and slightly lower price points.

FAQ

What should buyers watch for?

Sewer lateral condition, cast iron drains, older electrical panels, tuck-pointing, and HVAC at end of life. Pre-war South City brick has a consistent inspection profile.
