Quick answers

Concord Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Short answers first. Each one links to the sales behind it further down the page.

How much do houses cost in Concord, Missouri?

In 56 sales inside the Census boundary of the Concord community (CDP) that closed between January 9 and October 1, 2026, the median sale price was $352,500. The middle half sold between $311,250 and $425,225, and the full range ran from $200,000 to $849,900.

That’s a different measure from the Census median owner value for Concord (about $303,600), which reflects owners’ estimates of all homes, not recent sales.

Can I buy a house in Concord for $300,000?

14 of 56 sales (25.0%) closed at $300,000 or less. Of those 14, 12 had three or more bedrooms, 9 had two or more baths and 7 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,412 sq ft, built in 1958.

What does $350,000 buy in Concord?

28 of 56 sales (50.0%) closed at $350,000 or less. Of those 28, 25 had three or more bedrooms, 23 had two or more baths and 16 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,527.5 sq ft, built in 1961.

How fast do houses sell in Concord?

The median sale spent 4.5 days on market (CDOM). A quarter of the 56 sales took 3 days or less; the slowest quarter took about 32 days or more. 16 sales took 30+ days, 6 took 60+ and the longest took 253.

Do Concord homes sell over asking?

Measured against the original list price, 34 of 56 (60.7%) closed above it, 5 (8.9%) at it and 17 (30.4%) below it. The median sale was 101.5% of the original ask. The $300,000–$349,999 range was the most competitive: 11 of its 13 sales closed above the original ask.

What kind of houses are common in Concord?

Mostly houses from the 1950s and 1960s. Of the 56 sales, 14 (25.0%) were built before 1960, 32 (57.1%) from 1960 to 1979, 5 (8.9%) from 1980 to 1999 and 5 (8.9%) in 2000 or later. The median house was about 1,801 sq ft, built in 1966, and 28 of 56 (50.0%) listed a ranch style.

Does a 63128 or 63123 address mean the house is in Concord?

No. The 56 sales inside the Concord boundary were in 63128 (40) and 63123 (16), and both ZIP codes reach well beyond Concord. MLS rarely says “Concord” at all: 45 of the 56 listed “St Louis” as the city, 7 “Unincorporated” and only 2 “Concord Village.” Check the St. Louis County GIS map for the exact address.

Which school district is Concord in?

Two. Of the 56 sales inside the Concord boundary, 39 were in the Lindbergh School District and 17 in Mehlville R-IX. Houses a few streets apart can be in different districts. Verify the assigned schools for the exact address before you write an offer.

Is Concord incorporated?

No. Concord is an unincorporated community in St. Louis County with no city government of its own, so County rules generally apply, including the occupancy permit required when a new occupant moves in after a sale. A mailing address alone doesn’t confirm that a property is unincorporated; check the St. Louis County GIS map.

A sale counts here when its listing’s map coordinates fall inside the Concord census-designated place (CDP), using the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2024 TIGER/Line place boundary. The same records feed the St. Louis County school district housing analysis.

Concord does not sell itself on trend. It sells itself on function. Buyers come here because the neighborhoods are established, the shopping and service access is easy, and the housing mix usually lands in a more approachable range than places with a flashier reputation.

The Census Bureau shows an 86% owner-occupied rate here, which is one of the cleanest signals that Concord is still primarily a place where people buy to live, stay, and maintain what they own.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters in Concord

Mehlville School District says it serves all or part of Concord, while Lindbergh Schools says it serves Concord Village. That overlap is part of why buyers need to verify boundaries by address instead of assuming one district covers every block. Day to day, Concord's bigger advantage is pure convenience: major South County retail, highway access, and routine errands all sit close at hand.

Home Rules

Concord Occupancy and Home Rules

Concord is an unincorporated community in St. Louis County, not a city with its own municipal government. A Concord mailing address does not by itself confirm that a property is unincorporated. County rules below apply when the property is within unincorporated St. Louis County. Check the St. Louis County GIS map or contact the County before relying on them.

Do I need an occupancy permit when I buy or rent a home in Concord?

If the property is in unincorporated St. Louis County, the County requires an occupancy permit when a new occupant moves in after a sale or rental change. The County’s application covers both sale and rental situations, and the new occupant must wait until the permit is issued before occupying the home.

See the County’s single-family dwelling occupancy application and permits and applications for current instructions.

Is there a sewer lateral repair program in Concord?

Unincorporated properties may be eligible for the St. Louis County Sewer Lateral Repair Program. Eligibility applies; assistance is not automatic. Check the County’s current program requirements and ordinance guidebook before budgeting for a repair.

Do I need a permit for a fence, deck, shed, or pool?

St. Louis County Transportation and Public Works handles applicable residential permits for unincorporated properties. The County’s ordinance guidebook describes these general rules:

  • Fence: A fence 6 feet or shorter generally does not require a building permit. Zoning, sight-distance, and private subdivision or property restrictions may still apply.
  • Deck: Permit and code review may apply, depending on the project. Confirm requirements for the specific address and work before construction.
  • Shed: A shed larger than 120 square feet requires a building permit.
  • Pool: Qualifying pools require a County permit and must meet applicable enclosure, setback, and electrical requirements.

Requirements can depend on the property and project details. Check with the County before work begins.

Are electrical, plumbing, or mechanical permits required?

County trade permits may apply to electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work. Check the County’s permits and applications for the project’s requirements.

Do Concord landlords need a rental license or registration?

A County occupancy permit is required when a new occupant moves in after a rental change at an unincorporated property. The available County information reviewed for this guide does not establish a separate local landlord license or registration requirement. Confirm with the County whether any additional rules apply to the property or rental arrangement.

Who handles property maintenance and code concerns?

For unincorporated properties, St. Louis County Neighborhood Preservation handles applicable property maintenance and code matters. Confirm that the address is in unincorporated County territory before contacting a city department.

How much does the occupancy permit cost?

The County’s downloadable occupancy application does not publish the current inspection fee. The fee must accompany the application, so confirm the current amount in the County permitting portal or with the County before submitting.

Before you buy, rent, or start a project: verify the property’s jurisdiction by exact address, then confirm current requirements with St. Louis County. Rules can differ for a property inside an incorporated municipality.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: St. Louis County Transportation and Public Works occupancy and permit pages; St. Louis County Planning ordinance guidebook. Requirements are address- and project-dependent.
