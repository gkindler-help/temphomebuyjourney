Cedar Hill Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records. 31 closed sales with a Cedar Hill mailing address.

How much does a house cost in Cedar Hill, Missouri?

The 2026 median sale price was $286,000 across 31 sales with a Cedar Hill mailing address, according to MLS sales records. The middle half sold between $241,700 and $324,500.

What does $250,000 buy in Cedar Hill?

9 of 31 sales (29.0%) closed at $250,000 or less. Of those, 5 had three or more bedrooms and 2 were ranch-style; the median was 1,132 sq ft, built in 1973.

How fast do homes sell in Cedar Hill?

The median 2026 sale spent 38 days on market (CDOM), against 18.5 days countywide. 17 of 31 sales took 30 days or more.

Do Cedar Hill homes sell over asking price?

What kind of houses sell in Cedar Hill?

The median 2026 sale was about 1,377 sq ft, built in 1993, and 10 of 31 (32.3%) were ranch-style.

Is Cedar Hill an incorporated city?

No. Cedar Hill is unincorporated Jefferson County, with no city government, according to the U.S. Census Bureau. County rules apply to homes with a Cedar Hill address.

How much are property taxes in Cedar Hill?

The median annual property tax on homes that sold with a Cedar Hill mailing address in 2026 was $2,281, and the middle half of bills ran from $1,487 to $3,541, across 31 sales, according to MLS sales records. The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Cedar Hill works best for buyers who understand what it is. It is a well-known northwest Jefferson County community where buyers often come looking for more land and a less compressed suburban pattern. That matters because people who shop this market usually are not buying a headline; they are buying fit, payment, and the way the location works Monday through Friday.

In Jefferson County, buyers are usually balancing distance, land, monthly cost, and pace. Cedar Hill fits when the buyer wants some separation from tighter county-line suburbs but still needs a location that feels workable, not remote.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

Cedar Hill sits in northwest jefferson county, and that location changes the lifestyle equation immediately. In Jefferson County, highway path, topography, and day-to-day errand distance all matter. Buyers who match the map to their real routine usually make better decisions here.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

What buyers actually find in Cedar Hill is a mix of older neighborhoods, scattered parcels, and semi-rural settings. The market is usually less about flash and more about whether the house solves the buyer’s real problem: payment, space, commute, or setting.

That is why Cedar Hill is usually best judged against House Springs, High Ridge, Byrnes Mill, not against inner-ring county suburbs with a totally different land pattern and pricing ladder. The right Jefferson County buy is often the one that gives you the most usable life for the payment.
