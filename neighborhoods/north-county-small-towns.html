FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About North County’s Small Towns

How much do houses cost in North County’s small towns?

Across the 111 sales inside these 28 towns’ boundaries in 2026, the median was $87,500. The middle half sold between $69,900 and $132,500, and prices ran from $12,000 to $439,900.

Do homes in these towns sell over asking?

Usually not. 75 of the 111 sales (67.6%) closed below the original list price, 16 at it and 20 above it. The median sale spent 32 days on market.

Which school districts serve these towns?

Several. Of the 111 2026 sales, 66 were in the Normandy Schools Collaborative, 15 were in the Ferguson-Florissant School District, 13 were in the Ritenour School District, 13 were in the Riverview Gardens School District and 4 were in the Jennings School District. The town name doesn’t settle the district, so verify it for the exact address.

Are these towns incorporated?

Almost all of them are separate cities, villages or towns with their own local rules. Castle Point is the exception: the Census Bureau lists it as an unincorporated community, so St. Louis County rules apply there. Occupancy and permit rules differ from town to town, so check with the city or village for the exact address.
