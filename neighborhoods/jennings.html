Jennings Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records. 33 closed sales inside Jennings city limits.

How much do houses cost in Jennings, Missouri?

In 33 sales inside Jennings city limits that closed between January 6 and September 25, 2026, the median sale price was $88,000. The middle half sold between $65,111 and $149,900, and the full range ran from $23,000 to $240,000.

Can I buy a house in Jennings for $100,000?

19 of 33 sales (57.6%) closed at $100,000 or less. Of those 19, 7 had three or more bedrooms and 10 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,026 sq ft, built in 1949.

How fast do homes sell in Jennings?

The median was 32 days on market (CDOM), with the middle half between 18 and 81 days. 18 of the 33 sales took 30 days or more.

Do homes in Jennings sell over asking price?

Some do, but most did not. Of the 33 sales, 7 closed above the original list price, 7 at it and 19 below it. The median sale was 99.0% of the original asking price.

How old are most homes in Jennings?

The median house was built in 1950 and had 1,176 sq ft. By year built, the 33 sales broke down this way: 16 before 1950, 15 from 1950 to 1969, 0 from 1970 to 1999 and 2 in 2000 or later. The median home had 3 bedrooms.

How much does a 2-bedroom house cost in Jennings?

In 2026, the 14 two-bedroom homes that sold inside Jennings city limits had a median price of $71,556; the middle half sold between $48,499 and $89,125. The median home had 1,002 sq ft, built in 1946.

How much does a 3-bedroom house cost in Jennings?

In 2026, the 12 three-bedroom homes that sold inside Jennings city limits had a median price of $123,975; the middle half sold between $78,250 and $162,250. The median home had 1,188 sq ft, built in 1954.

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Jennings?

In 2026, the 7 homes with four or more bedrooms that sold inside Jennings city limits had a median price of $183,000, with prices from $57,000 to $220,000. The median home had 1,508 sq ft, built in 1952.

How much does a ranch house cost in Jennings?

In 2026, the 18 ranch-style homes that sold inside Jennings city limits had a median price of $93,450; the middle half sold between $71,250 and $146,425. The median home had 3 bedrooms and 1,270 sq ft, built in 1951.

How much does a bungalow cost in Jennings?

In 2026, the 7 bungalows that sold inside Jennings city limits had a median price of $70,000, with prices from $23,000 to $124,950. The median home had 2 bedrooms and 918 sq ft, built in 1940.

How much does a house with 2 bathrooms cost in Jennings?

In 2026, the 17 homes with two or more bathrooms that sold inside Jennings city limits had a median price of $123,000; the middle half sold between $82,900 and $189,900. The median home had 3 bedrooms and 1,436 sq ft, built in 1953.

Which school district is Jennings in?

All 33 sales inside Jennings city limits were in the Jennings School District. District lines don’t always follow city limits, so verify the assigned schools for the exact address before you write an offer.

How much are property taxes in Jennings?

In 2026 sales inside Jennings city limits, the median annual property tax was $1,736, about 1.70% of the sale price. The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so use this median to compare areas, then confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue before you make an offer.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Jennings works best for buyers who understand what it is. It is an established inner North County municipality that stays relevant for buyers focused on payment, access, and housing utility. That matters because people who shop this market usually are not buying a headline; they are buying fit, payment, and the way the location works Monday through Friday.

In North County, the strongest pages are the honest ones. Jennings tends to reward buyers who want established housing and practical value instead of prestige branding. The right buy here is usually about condition, block, and commute pattern more than trend.

Location & Identity

Why the Map Matters Here

Jennings sits in inner north county, which means buyers usually evaluate it through the lens of access, block-to-block feel, and nearby service corridors. That is why comparing it only by city name misses the point. In this part of the metro, the micro-location often matters as much as the municipality itself.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

What buyers actually find in Jennings is older homes in a compact grid with a traditional inner-ring feel. The market is usually less about flash and more about whether the house solves the buyer’s real problem: payment, space, commute, or setting.

That is why Jennings is usually best judged against Dellwood, Normandy, Pine Lawn, not against the metro’s highest-status suburbs. Buyers who shop it on its own terms often find more house-for-the-money than they expected.

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Jennings School District in 2026 was $1,718, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $1,273 to $1,969, across 38 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.
