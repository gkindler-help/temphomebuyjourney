Schools

Schools in Mehlville

The Mehlville neighborhood sits inside the Mehlville School District. The district is large, and buyer demand inside it is not uniform — Oakville typically pulls stronger competition than the Mehlville core. That can work in your favor here: buyers who are willing to be in the Mehlville attendance area instead of Oakville often find more negotiating room and less weekend-offer pressure. The Honest Take

Practical

Mehlville is one of those names buyers use in two different ways. Sometimes they mean the census-defined community. Sometimes they mean the larger South County school-district world around it. Either way, the appeal is similar: established housing, practical road access, and a daily-life setup that works.

The Mehlville School District describes itself as serving all or part of Mehlville, Oakville, Concord, and Lemay across about 42 square miles. That district identity is part of why 'Mehlville' carries more weight in conversation than the CDP boundaries alone would suggest.

Market Reality

Compared with Concord or Oakville, Mehlville often reads as a more value-driven option. Compared with Lemay, it can feel a little broader and more suburban. That in-between position is exactly why it stays relevant for many buyers.

What zip codes are in Mehlville?

The Mehlville CDP primarily falls within the 63125 zip code, though the broader Mehlville School District serves parts of 63123, 63128, and 63129 across Concord, Oakville, and Lemay.

FAQ

What is the median home price in Mehlville?

Census data puts the median owner-occupied home value in Mehlville around $218,400 — a practical mid-range position within South County.

FAQ

Is Mehlville incorporated?

No. Mehlville is an unincorporated community in South St. Louis County. It does not have its own city government — the Mehlville name is most prominently associated with the school district.
