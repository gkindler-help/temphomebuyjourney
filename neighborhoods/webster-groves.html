Webster Groves Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

How much do houses cost in Webster Groves, Missouri?

In 82 sales inside Webster Groves city limits that closed between January 5 and September 29, 2026, the median sale price was $444,500. The middle half sold between $348,125 and $645,500, and the full range ran from $173,000 to $1,675,000.

What does $400,000 buy in Webster Groves?

32 of 82 sales (39.0%) closed at $400,000 or less. Of those 32, 16 had three or more bedrooms, 14 had two or more baths and 15 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,220.5 sq ft, built in 1949.

What does $500,000 buy in Webster Groves?

How fast do houses sell in Webster Groves?

The median sale spent 5 days on market (CDOM). A quarter of the 82 sales took 3 days or less; the slowest quarter took about 27 days or more. 20 sales took 30+ days, 9 took 60+ and the longest took 274.

Do Webster Groves homes sell over asking?

Measured against the original list price, 51 of 82 (62.2%) closed above it, 4 (4.9%) at it and 27 (32.9%) below it. The median sale was 101.7% of the original ask. Under $300,000, 11 of 15 closed below it. From $700,000 to $999,999, all 10 closed above it.

What kind of houses are common in Webster Groves?

Mostly houses built before 1960, many before World War II. Of the 82 sales, 73 (89.0%) were built before 1960, 4 (4.9%) from 1960 to 1979 and 5 (6.1%) from 1980 to 1999. The median house was about 1,936 sq ft, built in 1936, and 25 of 82 (30.5%) listed a ranch style.

Does a 63119 address mean the house is in Webster Groves?

Not on its own. All 82 sales inside Webster Groves city limits were in 63119, but 63119 also covers neighboring areas. 63 listed “Webster Groves” as the city in MLS and 19 listed “St Louis.” Check the St. Louis County GIS map for the exact address.

Methodology

A sale counts here when its listing’s map coordinates fall inside the City of Webster Groves’ boundary, using the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2024 TIGER/Line place boundary. The same records feed the St. Louis County school district housing analysis.

The Honest Take

Village-Style Appeal With Real Municipal Depth

Webster Groves works because it feels layered. It is not just one corridor or one subdivision era. The city's own history traces Webster Groves back to five separate communities that merged in 1896 to form one city.

That origin still matters. Buyers experience Webster Groves as a collection of smaller, recognizable places — Old Webster, Old Orchard, and the surrounding residential neighborhoods — rather than as one flat suburban map.

The result is a suburb that feels more textured and more walkable in pockets than many buyers expect when they first broaden their search into the county.

Location & Identity

Old Webster, Old Orchard, and a Multi-Core Feel

City history explains that Webster, Old Orchard, Webster Park, Tuxedo Park, and Selma merged in 1896. Modern city materials still reflect that structure by promoting Old Webster and Old Orchard events and describing Webster Groves as a place with multiple business and community centers.

The city's tourism pages also call Webster Groves a culinary destination, which reinforces the suburb's identity as more than purely residential.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

Webster Groves buyers tend to be looking for an older suburb with actual neighborhoods, not just a large municipal boundary. That usually means mature trees, older housing stock, and a stronger sense of district identity.

It also means buyers should compare block by block. Some homes trade bigger lots for less walkability; others trade size for proximity to the business districts that make Webster feel like Webster.

In a metro full of suburbs, that kind of internal variety is one of Webster Groves' real strengths.

Home Rules

Webster Groves Occupancy and Home Rules

Webster Groves is an incorporated city in St. Louis County with its own municipal government. A Webster Groves mailing address or ZIP code does not by itself confirm that a property is inside city limits. City rules below apply when the property is within Webster Groves city limits. Check the St. Louis County GIS map or contact the City before relying on them.

Do I need an occupancy permit when I buy or rent a home in Webster Groves?

Yes. A passed residential occupancy inspection is required before an occupancy permit, and both purchase and rental are application types. See the City’s occupancy permits page.

After inspection, the City issues a Certificate of Compliance; the new owner or tenant then separately obtains an occupancy permit before moving in. See housing inspections.

Is there a sewer lateral repair program in Webster Groves?

Yes. The City’s sewer lateral program covers eligible single-family homes, duplexes, and apartment developments up to 6 units. The City pays 80% of the lowest approved bid, up to $3,500, or up to $8,000 if the repair is in the street.

Do I need a permit for a fence, deck, shed, or pool?

City Planning & Development administers building permits, with plan and zoning review. Some exterior work may require Architectural Review Board approval.

  • Fence: A permit is required for all new and replacement fences; repairs below a defined threshold are exempt.
  • Deck: A building permit is required for applicable deck construction.
  • Shed: Accessory structures and building alterations are subject to City building and zoning review.
  • Pool: Pool and hot-tub permits are available; City code requirements apply.

Requirements can depend on the property and project details. Check with the City before work begins.

Are electrical, plumbing, or mechanical permits required?

The City of Webster Groves issues and administers electrical, plumbing, and mechanical permits. Check the City’s building permits page for the project’s requirements.

Do Webster Groves landlords need a rental license or registration?

Rental is an occupancy application type, so an occupancy permit is required before a tenant moves in. Residential occupancy rules apply, and apartments are inspected annually.

Who handles property maintenance and code concerns?

The City of Webster Groves. Its Property Maintenance Code applies, and the occupancy inspection enforces violations before occupancy.

How much does the occupancy permit cost?

The occupancy inspection is $75, and the occupancy permit is $30.

Before you buy, rent, or start a project: verify that the property is inside Webster Groves city limits by exact address, then confirm current requirements with the City of Webster Groves.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: City of Webster Groves occupancy permits, housing inspections, sewer lateral program, building permits, fence permits, and pool and hot tub. Requirements are address- and project-dependent.
