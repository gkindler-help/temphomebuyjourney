Is a Jefferson County Eureka address inside Eureka city limits?

Part of it. The City of Eureka reaches across the Meramec River into Jefferson County, and that part is mostly the Windswept Farms subdivision, which the city voluntarily annexed in 2019. On the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2024 map, 11 of the 23 sales in 2026 were inside city limits, all in Windswept Farms, and two newer homes next to it had tax bills in line with the city homes. Older subdivisions such as Mirasol Manor and the Northwest R-I homes are unincorporated Jefferson County. The taxing bodies on the Jefferson County assessor’s parcel record settle it for a specific house.

Does living inside Eureka city limits raise your property tax?

Yes. In 2026, the Windswept Farms homes inside Eureka city limits paid about $10.50 to $14.30 a year in property tax for every $1,000 of sale price.
