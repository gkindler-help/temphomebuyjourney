Schools

Which school district is Crystal Lake Park in?

The Ladue School District, which covers the entire city of Crystal Lake Park. Recent Crystal Lake Park listings were assigned to Ladue Horton Watkins High School.

⚠ Confirm the assigned schools for the exact address with the district before you write an offer.

The Honest Take

What is Crystal Lake Park like?

A small residential city, not a neighborhood inside a larger one. It has no downtown or commercial corridor of its own; it’s quiet streets of houses, with shopping and services in the surrounding communities.

The housing is mostly 1960s–1980s ranches and split-levels, and vinyl siding is common. With about 221 homes, only a handful sell in a typical year, so be ready when one that fits comes up.

FAQ

Where is Crystal Lake Park?

In west-central St. Louis County, in the 63131 ZIP code. It’s a small incorporated city of about 508 residents (2020 Census), surrounded by larger West County communities.

FAQ

Is Crystal Lake Park in the Kirkwood School District?

No. The entire city is in the Ladue School District, even though it shares the 63131 ZIP code with addresses in other districts. Confirm the assigned schools for the exact address with the district.
