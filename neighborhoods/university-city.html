How much do houses cost in University City, Missouri?

In 88 sales inside the City of University City’s Census boundary that closed between January 12 and September 30, 2026, the median sale price was $439,200. The middle half sold between $195,500 and $644,750, and the full range ran from $35,000 to $2,275,000.

Can I buy a house in University City for $300,000?

31 of 88 sales (35.2%) closed at $300,000 or less. Of those 31, 17 had three or more bedrooms, 11 had two or more baths and 11 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,170 sq ft, built in 1941.

What does $500,000 buy in University City?

49 of 88 sales (55.7%) closed at $500,000 or less. Of those 49, 31 had three or more bedrooms, 27 had two or more baths and 14 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,344.5 sq ft, built in 1939.

How fast do houses sell in University City?

The median sale spent 20 days on market (CDOM). A quarter of the 88 sales took 4 days or less; the slowest quarter took about 46 days or more. 36 sales took 30+ days, 15 took 60+ and the longest took 221.

Do University City homes sell over asking?

Measured against the original list price, 39 of 88 (44.3%) closed above it, 12 (13.6%) at it and 37 (42.0%) below it. The median sale was 100.0% of the original ask.

What kind of houses are common in University City?

Mostly older houses. Of the 88 sales, 81 (92.0%) were built before 1960, 2 (2.3%) from 1980 to 1999 and 5 (5.7%) in 2000 or later. The median house was about 1,815 sq ft, built in 1935, and 15 of 88 (17.0%) listed a ranch style.

Does a University City mailing address mean the house is in University City?

Not necessarily. The 88 sales inside the city boundary were in 63130 (82), 63132 (5) and 63105 (1), and ZIP codes don’t follow city limits. 66 of the 88 listed “University City” as the city in MLS and 22 listed “St Louis.” Check the St. Louis County GIS map for the exact address.

Is all of University City in the School District of University City?

All 88 sales inside the city boundary this year were in the School District of University City. District lines don’t always follow city limits, so verify the assigned schools for the exact address before you write an offer.

How much are property taxes in University City?

In 2026 sales inside University City, the median annual property tax was $4,987, about 1.10% of the sale price. All of those sales were in the School District of University City. The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so use this median to compare areas, then confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue before you make an offer.

Is University City its own city?

Yes. University City is an incorporated city in St. Louis County with its own city government. Before you close, ask the city what it requires when a house changes hands, such as an occupancy permit or inspection.

Methodology

How This University City Analysis Was Built

This analysis uses 88 closed-sale records from the STL Home Journey 2026 St. Louis County sales dataset (January 12–September 30, 2026). A sale counts here when its listing’s map coordinates fall inside the Census Bureau’s boundary for the City of University City, using the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2024 TIGER/Line place boundary. The same records feed the St. Louis County school district housing analysis.

  • Historical closed sales are not current inventory.
  • Days on market uses CDOM.
  • Sale ÷ original ask compares the close price with the original list price.
  • Ranch share counts any MLS style that includes “Ranch.”
  • Listing coordinates can be imprecise near the boundary. Verify jurisdiction and school assignment for the exact address.

The Honest Take

Not One Thing, Which Is Exactly the Point

University City is too large and too varied to reduce to one vibe. That is actually its advantage. Buyers can search here for a more central county location, for specific historic pockets, or for proximity to the Delmar Loop and Washington University area.

Recent city materials cite a 2020 population of 35,065 and describe the city as roughly six square miles. That scale creates real internal variety, which is why buyers need to choose subareas intentionally rather than talking about University City as one uniform experience.

The upside is flexibility. There are few inner-ring cities where you can get this much neighborhood range without leaving one municipal boundary.

Location & Identity

The Loop Changes the Way the City Is Read

University City's community profile describes the University City Loop on Delmar Boulevard as a five-block area with restaurants, galleries, specialty shops, the Tivoli, and the St. Louis Walk of Fame. That commercial identity gives the city regional pull beyond its residential base.

At the same time, city and financial documents describe University City as a stable residential community adjacent to Washington University, with a large variety of housing types.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

The biggest mistake buyers make in University City is assuming all of it behaves the same. It does not. Some pockets are quieter and more residential; others are more mixed, denser, or more tied to major corridors.

That makes comparison work essential. Buyers should decide whether they care more about lot, architecture, school options, commercial proximity, or simple centrality — because University City can deliver different versions of value depending on where you shop.

For flexible buyers, that is a strength. For unfocused buyers, it can feel confusing.
