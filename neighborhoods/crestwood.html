Crestwood Home Buying Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Short answers first. Each one links to the sales behind it further down the page.

How much do houses cost in Crestwood, Missouri?

In 40 sales inside Crestwood city limits that closed between February 10 and September 28, 2026, the median sale price was $365,000. The middle half sold between $312,000 and $428,250, and the full range ran from $185,000 to $775,000.

That’s a different measure from the Census median owner value for Crestwood (about $319,600), which reflects owners’ estimates of all homes, not recent sales.

What does $300,000 buy in Crestwood?

9 of 40 sales (22.5%) closed at $300,000 or less. Of those 9, 6 had three or more bedrooms, 6 had two or more baths and 6 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,294 sq ft, built in 1956.

What does $400,000 buy in Crestwood?

26 of 40 sales (65.0%) closed at $400,000 or less. Of those 26, 23 had three or more bedrooms, 21 had two or more baths and 21 were ranch-style. Their median was 1,369.5 sq ft, built in 1956.

How fast do houses sell in Crestwood?

The median sale spent 6.5 days on market (CDOM). A quarter of the 40 sales took 4 days or less; the slowest quarter took about 20 days or more. 9 sales took 30+ days, 2 took 60+ and the longest took 100.

Do Crestwood homes sell over asking?

Measured against the original list price, 26 of 40 (65.0%) closed above it, 4 (10.0%) at it and 10 (25.0%) below it. The median sale was 103.4% of the original ask. From $350,000 up, 17 of 22 sales closed above the original ask and only 1 closed below it.

What kind of houses are common in Crestwood?

Mostly 1950s ranches. Of the 40 sales, 25 (62.5%) were built before 1960, 11 (27.5%) from 1960 to 1979, 3 (7.5%) from 1980 to 1999 and 1 (2.5%) in 2000 or later. The median house was about 1,646 sq ft, built in 1957, and 29 of 40 (72.5%) listed a ranch style.

Does a Crestwood address mean the house is inside Crestwood city limits?

Not necessarily, and the reverse is true too. Of the 40 sales inside Crestwood city limits, only 20 listed “Crestwood” as the city in MLS; the other 20 listed “St Louis.” They were in ZIP codes 63126 (35) and 63123 (5), which also cover areas outside the city. Check the St. Louis County GIS map for the exact address.

Which school district is Crestwood in?

Mostly Lindbergh. Of the 40 sales inside Crestwood city limits in 2026, 39 were in the Lindbergh School District and 1 in Affton. Addresses near the city’s edges can differ. Verify the assigned schools for the exact address before you write an offer.

Does Crestwood require an occupancy inspection when I buy?

Not for an owner-occupied home. Crestwood doesn’t require an occupancy inspection or occupancy permit when an owner-occupied home is sold. Rentals are different: a rental re-occupancy permit and a passed inspection are required before anyone moves in.

Methodology

How This Crestwood Analysis Was Built

This analysis uses 40 closed-sale records from the STL Home Journey 2026 St. Louis County sales dataset (February 10–September 28, 2026). A sale counts here when its listing’s map coordinates fall inside the City of Crestwood’s boundary, using the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2024 TIGER/Line place boundary. The same records feed the St. Louis County school district housing analysis.

  • Historical closed sales are not current inventory.
  • Days on market uses CDOM.
  • Sale ÷ original ask compares the close price with the original list price.
  • Ranch share counts any MLS style that includes “Ranch.”
  • Listing coordinates can be imprecise near the boundary. Verify jurisdiction and school assignment for the exact address.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Crestwood tends to attract buyers who want South County credibility without the price point of Sunset Hills. The city feels settled and municipal rather than loosely suburban. That difference matters to buyers who care about identity, upkeep, and neighborhood consistency.

Census data puts Crestwood's owner-occupied rate close to 88%, and the city's parks department says it manages six parks across 120 acres along with the community center, aquatic center, and the historic Sappington House. That combination of ownership and civic infrastructure is a big part of Crestwood's appeal.

Home Rules

Crestwood Occupancy and Home Rules

Crestwood is an incorporated city in St. Louis County with its own municipal government. A Crestwood mailing address or ZIP code does not by itself confirm that a property is inside city limits. City rules below apply when the property is within Crestwood city limits. Check the St. Louis County GIS map or contact the City before relying on them.

Do I need an occupancy permit when I buy or rent a home in Crestwood?

Crestwood does not require an occupancy inspection or occupancy permit when an owner-occupied home is sold.

Rentals are different: a residential rental re-occupancy permit is required for all rental dwelling types, and the property cannot be occupied until the inspection passes with no deficiencies. Submit the application at least 30 days before the proposed occupancy. See the City’s permits and licenses page for current instructions.

Is there a sewer lateral repair program in Crestwood?

Crestwood has a City residential sewer-lateral program, and current program terms apply. Read the City’s sewer lateral repair policy before budgeting for a repair.

Do I need a permit for a fence, deck, shed, or pool?

In Crestwood, City zoning review comes first; St. Louis County permits and inspections follow as applicable. The City’s permits and licenses page describes these general rules:

  • Fence: A new fence requires a City permit.
  • Deck: Regulated; the City/County permit path depends on the project.
  • Shed: Small non-electrified sheds have a City-only permit path; larger or electrified sheds also need a County permit.
  • Pool: Regulated; confirm the project path with the City.

Requirements can depend on the property and project details. Check with the City before work begins.

Are electrical, plumbing, or mechanical permits required?

County permits apply to applicable electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work after City zoning review. Check the City’s permits and licenses page for the project’s requirements.

Do Crestwood landlords need a rental license or registration?

Crestwood requires a rental re-occupancy permit. It applies to single-family homes, villas, condos, apartments, and similar rentals, and only named occupants may occupy the unit.

Who handles property maintenance and code concerns?

The City of Crestwood handles property maintenance and code matters under its municipal code.

How much does the occupancy permit cost?

The rental re-occupancy inspection is $340, with a $175 reinspection fee. Owner-occupied resales do not require a City occupancy permit.

Before you buy, rent, or start a project: verify that the property is inside Crestwood city limits by exact address, then confirm current requirements with the City of Crestwood and St. Louis County where they apply.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: City of Crestwood permits and licenses page, sewer lateral repair policy, and municipal code. Requirements are address- and project-dependent.
