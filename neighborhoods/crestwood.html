Schools

Schools in Crestwood

Crestwood spans two district boundaries, and that split matters for buyers. Most of Crestwood falls inside Lindbergh Schools — one of the stronger South County district names, which is a meaningful part of why Crestwood gets consistent buyer demand. Some addresses fall inside Kirkwood School District, which carries its own premium. The district assignment can shift the buyer pool, the competition level, and the resale story for an otherwise comparable house. Verify the address before you assume which district applies.

⚠ School boundaries can vary by exact property address. Verify assigned schools directly with the district, county records, or your buyer agent before writing an offer.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Crestwood tends to attract buyers who want South County credibility without the price point of Sunset Hills. The city feels settled and municipal rather than loosely suburban. That difference matters to buyers who care about identity, upkeep, and neighborhood consistency.

Census data puts Crestwood's owner-occupied rate close to 88%, and the city's parks department says it manages six parks across 120 acres along with the community center, aquatic center, and the historic Sappington House. That combination of ownership and civic infrastructure is a big part of Crestwood's appeal.

Home Rules

Crestwood Occupancy and Home Rules

Crestwood is an incorporated city in St. Louis County with its own municipal government. A Crestwood mailing address or ZIP code does not by itself confirm that a property is inside city limits. City rules below apply when the property is within Crestwood city limits. Check the St. Louis County GIS map or contact the City before relying on them.

Do I need an occupancy permit when I buy or rent a home in Crestwood?

Crestwood does not require an occupancy inspection or occupancy permit when an owner-occupied home is sold.

Rentals are different: a residential rental re-occupancy permit is required for all rental dwelling types, and the property cannot be occupied until the inspection passes with no deficiencies. Submit the application at least 30 days before the proposed occupancy. See the City’s permits and licenses page for current instructions.

Is there a sewer lateral repair program in Crestwood?

Crestwood has a City residential sewer-lateral program, and current program terms apply. Read the City’s sewer lateral repair policy before budgeting for a repair.

Do I need a permit for a fence, deck, shed, or pool?

In Crestwood, City zoning review comes first; St. Louis County permits and inspections follow as applicable. The City’s permits and licenses page describes these general rules:

  • Fence: A new fence requires a City permit.
  • Deck: Regulated; the City/County permit path depends on the project.
  • Shed: Small non-electrified sheds have a City-only permit path; larger or electrified sheds also need a County permit.
  • Pool: Regulated; confirm the project path with the City.

Requirements can depend on the property and project details. Check with the City before work begins.

Are electrical, plumbing, or mechanical permits required?

County permits apply to applicable electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work after City zoning review. Check the City’s permits and licenses page for the project’s requirements.

Do Crestwood landlords need a rental license or registration?

Crestwood requires a rental re-occupancy permit. It applies to single-family homes, villas, condos, apartments, and similar rentals, and only named occupants may occupy the unit.

Who handles property maintenance and code concerns?

The City of Crestwood handles property maintenance and code matters under its municipal code.

How much does the occupancy permit cost?

The rental re-occupancy inspection is $340, with a $175 reinspection fee. Owner-occupied resales do not require a City occupancy permit.

Before you buy, rent, or start a project: verify that the property is inside Crestwood city limits by exact address, then confirm current requirements with the City of Crestwood and St. Louis County where they apply.

Sources checked September 25, 2026: City of Crestwood permits and licenses page, sewer lateral repair policy, and municipal code. Requirements are address- and project-dependent.
