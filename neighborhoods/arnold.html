How much does a house cost in Arnold, Missouri?

The 2026 median sale price was $305,000 across 97 sales with an Arnold mailing address, according to MLS sales records. The middle half sold between $253,000 and $395,000.

What kind of houses sell in Arnold?

Is an Arnold address inside Arnold city limits?

Not always. Of the 97 sales in 2026 with an Arnold mailing address, 55 were inside Arnold city limits and 42 were outside, according to U.S. Census Bureau 2024 boundaries. Inside the city, the city’s rules apply.

Do I need an occupancy inspection to sell a house in Arnold?

Yes, if the house is inside Arnold city limits. The City of Arnold requires a property maintenance inspection and a certificate of compliance before a home is sold or a new occupant moves in. Arnold-address homes outside city limits fall under Jefferson County, which has no resale inspection of its own, though Rock Community Fire Protection District inspects homes in its area at a change of ownership.

Deciding on a house in Arnold

How fast do I have to move on a house in Arnold?

Fast. Arnold is one of the quickest markets in Jefferson County: the median Arnold-address sale went under contract in 8 days in 2026, and 51 of 97 closed above the original asking price. Homes that sold inside 30 days closed at a median 101.0% of asking. Have your preapproval and your buyer agreement settled before the first showing, not after.

Is it cheaper to buy inside Arnold city limits or just outside them?

A little cheaper outside. In 2026, the 55 sales inside Arnold city limits had a $310,000 median and the 42 Arnold-address sales outside had a $283,500 median, while median property tax was nearly identical ($1,741 inside, $1,756 outside). The bigger difference shows up when you sell: inside the city, Arnold’s property maintenance inspection applies.

Arnold houses run from the 1960s to the 2000s. What repairs should I plan for?

43 of the 97 Arnold sales were built before 1980, and that older group is where the expensive surprises sit, especially the sewer lateral. Replacement typically runs $4,000 to $12,000 and can exceed $15,000 in some cases. Price a specific house before you write the offer, not after the inspection.

Schools

Schools in Arnold, Missouri

Arnold is served by the Fox C-6 School District. In 2026, all 97 sales with an Arnold mailing address were in Fox C-6. School district assignment affects buyer demand and long-term resale — buyers with school-age children, or anyone thinking ahead to resale, should verify which district covers the specific address before committing. District lines don't always follow neighborhood or city boundaries.

The Honest Take

What Buyers Actually Need to Understand

Arnold works best for buyers who understand what it is. It is one of the largest and most recognizable Jefferson County search targets, especially for buyers trying to balance commute practicality with lower housing cost than inner-ring county suburbs. That matters because people who shop this market usually are not buying a headline; they are buying fit, payment, and the way the location works Monday through Friday.

In Jefferson County, buyers are usually balancing distance, land, monthly cost, and pace. Arnold fits when the buyer wants some separation from tighter county-line suburbs but still needs a location that feels workable, not remote.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

What buyers actually find in Arnold is a broad mix of established subdivisions, townhome product, and everyday suburban inventory. The market is usually less about flash and more about whether the house solves the buyer’s real problem: payment, space, commute, or setting.

That is why Arnold is usually best judged against Imperial, Barnhart, Oakville, not against inner-ring county suburbs with a totally different land pattern and pricing ladder. The right Jefferson County buy is often the one that gives you the most usable life for the payment.
