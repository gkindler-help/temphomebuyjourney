The Park

Tower Grove Park — Same Park. Different Address.

Tower Grove Park is 289 acres — one of the most ornate Victorian parks in the country. The eastern border of that park is Tower Grove East. The southern border is Tower Grove South. Tower Grove South charges a premium for its address; Tower Grove East doesn't. The park experience from either side is the same park.

For buyers who walk Tower Grove Park on a Saturday morning and then compare listing prices on both sides: the price gap between Tower Grove South and Tower Grove East is real, and it is explained almost entirely by which neighborhood name buyers search for. The park doesn't have an address preference. The market does. That's the opportunity.

Schools

Schools in Tower Grove East

Tower Grove East is served by St. Louis Public Schools (SLPS). Same district as Tower Grove South — the school district doesn't explain the price gap between the two neighborhoods. The gap is explained by name recognition only. For buyers using SLPS or private school, that makes Tower Grove East's lower price point a straightforward win.

⚠ Always verify assigned school by exact property address before making any school-driven decisions.

Market Reality

What Buyers Actually Find Here

Brick flats, bungalows, and some two-story brick homes from the early-to-mid 20th century. Better average condition than Dutchtown or Gravois Park — Tower Grove East has benefited from renovation activity driven by proximity to the park and South Grand. The rehabbed inventory is genuinely nice. The as-is end exists but is less common.

Standard South City inspection checklist applies: sewer lateral, older electrical on non-updated homes, cast iron drains, tuck-pointing, HVAC. Budget $5K–$15K depending on update level. Scope the sewer before closing on anything — it remains the highest-stakes unknown in South City regardless of condition tier.

FAQ

Where is Tower Grove East?

Tower Grove East is in South St. Louis City, bordering the eastern side of Tower Grove Park. It is adjacent to Tower Grove South and within walking distance of the South Grand corridor.

FAQ

How does Tower Grove East compare to Tower Grove South?

Same park. Same school district. South Grand access from both. Tower Grove South prices higher based on name recognition and the specific streets along the southern border. The park doesn't care which side you're on.

FAQ

Is South Grand walkable from Tower Grove East?

Yes. The South Grand restaurant and retail corridor is within walking distance from most of Tower Grove East — one of the neighborhood's strongest lifestyle assets alongside the park.

FAQ

What school district serves Tower Grove East?

St. Louis Public Schools (SLPS) — same as Tower Grove South. The school district doesn't explain the price gap between the two neighborhoods. Name recognition does.

FAQ

What should buyers watch for?

Sewer lateral condition, older electrical on non-updated homes, cast iron drains, tuck-pointing, HVAC. Better average condition than the southern South City corridor — but the checklist still applies and the sewer scope is non-negotiable before closing.
