Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

STL Home Journey is run by George Kindler, a licensed Missouri real estate agent with The Closing Pros LLC. This page explains what the site collects when you use it, why, and what it doesn’t do. The short version: I never sell your information.

What information do you send me?

Only what you choose to send. The site has no sign-up forms and no accounts. If you call, text or email me, I get what you send: your name, number or email address and your message. I use it to answer you and to help with your home search or sale. I don’t add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

The “Chat with George” button opens a live chat run by Tawk.to. Nothing from Tawk.to loads until you tap the button. Once you do, the messages and any details you type go through Tawk.to so I can read and answer them, along with the page you’re on and its topic. Tawk.to may set its own cookies to keep the conversation going.

What does the site measure?

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and tools help people. Google Analytics sets cookies and records things like the pages you view, how long you stay, the kind of device and browser you use, your general location (city level) and the site that sent you. Google may process that data on its own servers. The site also sends a few events of its own:

  • the first time you change each calculator or tool on a page (which tool and which page, not the numbers you type);
  • when you click a link that leaves the site (where it goes and the link text);
  • when you click a post link on the Instagram or Facebook pages.

Analytics only runs on stlhomejourney.com itself. The numbers you enter into calculators are not sent to Google Analytics.

You can block it. Google offers a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics, and most browsers let you block or clear cookies. The site works the same either way.

What stays on your phone or computer?

Some tools save your inputs in your own browser so they’re still there when you come back: the house walk-through tool, the buying power calculator, the home buying journey checklist and a few others. That data stays on your device. It isn’t sent to me or anyone else, and clearing your browser’s site data deletes it.

Which outside services load on the site?

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google.
  • Tawk.to runs the live chat, and only loads if you tap “Chat with George.”
  • YouTube videos are embedded on a few pages. YouTube may set its own cookies when you play one.
  • Map tools load map tiles from CARTO and a mapping library from Cloudflare’s CDN. If you type an address into a map search, that address is sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to find it on the map.

Each of these companies has its own privacy policy.

Do you sell or share my information?

No. I don’t sell, rent or trade your personal information, and I don’t pass your contact details to lenders, call centers or other agents. If you ask me to connect you with a lender, inspector or other professional, I share only what you tell me to share.

Is the site meant for children?

No. The site is for adults buying, selling or researching homes, and I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

How will I know if this changes?

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
