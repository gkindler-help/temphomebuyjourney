About This Dataset

Over my career, I’ve been involved in more than 250 residential real estate transactions across the St. Louis region. I’ve been able to reconstruct and document more than 200 of those transactions individually through MLS and other records.

Earlier in my career, I also worked within the Realty Shop team structure, where I played mentorship, support, and transaction roles on additional deals that were recorded under other agents or team leadership. I include that experience in my career total, but I do not present those transactions individually here because I can no longer reliably reconstruct every property and transaction record.

Where a transaction is displayed on this site, I identify my role as accurately as possible—including whether I represented the client directly, worked in partnership with another agent, or participated in another capacity.
