Free St. Louis Calculators, Answered

Finding a tool

What free real estate calculators are there for St. Louis?

Seven, all free with no sign-up: a buying power calculator, a seller net calculator, the Before You Offer check, a home walk-through tool for repair costs, a cash offer decoder, a physician home plan and a neighborhood quiz. Each one uses St. Louis data rather than national averages.

Which St. Louis calculator should I start with?

Buying: start with buying power to see your price and where it reaches, then run Before You Offer on a specific house. Selling: start with the seller net calculator, and use the cash offer decoder if an investor made you an offer.

Trusting the numbers

Where does the calculators’ data come from?

MARIS closed sales for prices (7,006 sales across 79 ZIP codes for buying power and the cash offer decoder; 2026 sales by school district for Before You Offer), 2026 St. Louis repair cost ranges for the walk-through tool, and real 2026 seller settlement statements for selling costs.

How accurate are these calculators?

They’re estimates built on past sales, so treat them as a starting point. A lender confirms what you can borrow, an appraisal or an agent’s analysis confirms value, and an inspection confirms what a house needs.

How These Tools Are Built

Every number comes from a source I can point to: MARIS closed sales for prices, the County and school district data on this site for taxes, and my own transactions for seller costs. Groups with fewer than five sales show only a count, and no tool publishes a list of individual sales. Results are estimates; a lender confirms what you can borrow, and an inspection confirms what a house needs.
