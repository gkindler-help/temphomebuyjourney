Select a district on the map to see its DESE performance data, neighborhoods, and affordability link.
How this map is built. District boundaries come from the U.S. Department of Education's NCES EDGE geographic data. Performance figures — the Annual Performance Report (APR) score, accreditation status, and graduation rate — come from the Missouri Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE), 2025 reporting. APR scores and accreditation status are shown as separate facts: DESE has stated it will not use 2025 APR scores to change accreditation classifications until after the 2026 report. Figures are provided for general information; verify current data with DESE or the district before making decisions. This tool does not rate or rank districts — it displays official state data.
