About George

George Kindler is a licensed Missouri real estate agent and creator of STL Home Journey, a consumer-education platform built on more than a decade of hands-on experience across the St. Louis housing market. He specializes in the South County and South City corridors, with documented work spanning 53 municipalities and communities from 2014 through the present.

George is a United States Marine Corps veteran who served as an LAV Crewman from 2007 to 2011. After his service, he earned a BS in Business Administration and a Business Analytics Certificate from the University of Phoenix, and has been working toward a Project Management certificate.

He also operates Kindler Property Services, an exterior cleaning and professional photography business serving St. Louis area homeowners and agents.
