St. Louis Mortgage Payments, Answered

What is the monthly payment on a $250,000 house in St. Louis?

About $2,141 a month with a conventional loan, 5% down and a 7% rate: $1,580 principal and interest, $233 property taxes, $208 insurance and $119 PMI. With FHA and 3.5% down it’s about $2,185; with VA and nothing down, about $2,141. Taxes and insurance here are estimates (1.12% and 1% of the price a year), so enter the listing’s tax bill and your insurance quote in the calculator above.

What is the monthly payment on a $300,000 house in St. Louis?

About $2,569 a month with a conventional loan and 5% down at 7%, $2,622 with FHA and 3.5% down, and $2,569 with VA and nothing down. Put 20% down on a conventional loan and PMI goes away: about $2,127 a month, but you’d need about $72,530 to close.

What is included in a monthly mortgage payment?

Four parts, often called PITI: principal and interest on the loan, property taxes, and homeowners insurance, with the taxes and insurance usually collected each month into an escrow account. Add mortgage insurance with less than 20% down on a conventional loan (PMI) or on any FHA loan (MIP). VA loans have no monthly mortgage insurance. HOA dues are paid to the association, but count them in the monthly cost too.

How much are property taxes on a St. Louis house?

It depends on the school district far more than the price. Across 22 St. Louis County districts the median 2026 bill was $3,624, with district medians from $1,446 to $11,638; in Jefferson County the median was $2,084. How much cash do I need to close?

Your down payment plus closing costs (usually 2% to 3% of the price in St. Louis), the first year of insurance, an escrow deposit and some prepaid interest. The calculator adds those up, less any seller credit. What’s in buyer closing costs →

How the Calculator Works

Conventional: PMI is added when you put less than 20% down. Auto uses typical yearly rates by down payment (0.75% under 5% down, 0.60% at 5%, 0.45% at 10%, 0.30% at 15%), but your credit score moves it, so type your lender’s quote when you have one. Minimum 3% down.

FHA: 1.75% upfront mortgage insurance is added to the loan, plus monthly mortgage insurance of 0.55% a year (0.50% with 5% or more down) on a 30-year loan. Minimum 3.5% down.

VA: no monthly mortgage insurance. The funding fee is added to the loan: 2.15% with less than 5% down on first use, 1.5% with 5% down, 1.25% with 10%, 3.3% under 5% down if you’ve used a VA loan before, and nothing if you’re exempt for a VA disability rating.

Seller credit: capped at what the loan allows: 6% of the price on FHA, 4% on VA plus normal closing costs, and 3% to 9% on conventional depending on the down payment. It can’t be more than your closing costs and prepaids.

Every number is an estimate. Your lender’s Loan Estimate has the exact figures, and the tax bill on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, not a promise of yours.
