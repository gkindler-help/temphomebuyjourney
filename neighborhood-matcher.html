Ask Me to Compare Them

The eight parts of St. Louis this quiz can match you to

The quiz weighs your budget, commute, stage of life and priorities against these eight areas. Here’s what each one is like, with 2026 sales, so you can compare them even before you take it. School district lines don’t follow these areas, so check the district for any exact address on the school district map.

  • South City

    Brick bungalows and flats from the 1910s to the 1940s, front porches and walkable business strips around Tower Grove Park. Every address is in the St. Louis Public Schools district and pays the City’s 1% earnings tax.

    South City guide → · Tower Grove South → · Lindenwood Park →

  • Central West End

    Walkable streets between Forest Park and the medical campus, with condos, townhouses and renovated older homes. A common pick for people working at Barnes-Jewish or WashU.

    Central West End → · Working at BJC →

  • Mid-County

    Kirkwood, Webster Groves, Maplewood, Clayton, University City and Ladue: older houses close to Clayton and the hospitals. 2026 medians ran from $295,000 in Maplewood to $1,430,000 in Ladue.

    Mid-County compared → · Kirkwood → · Webster Groves →

  • South County

    Brick ranches and split-levels, much of it unincorporated. 2026 medians ran from $195,000 in Lemay to $729,050 in Sunset Hills, with Mehlville at $323,500 and Oakville at $385,000.

    South County guide → · Oakville → · Mehlville →

  • West County

    Larger, newer suburbs: Wildwood’s median house was built in 1992 and Chesterfield’s in 1982. 2026 medians ran from $304,000 in Valley Park to $1,348,000 in Town and Country.

    West County compared → · Chesterfield → · Ballwin →

  • St. Charles County

    Across the Missouri River along I-70 and Highway 364: newer subdivisions in O’Fallon, St. Peters, Wentzville and Lake St. Louis, under a separate county government with its own tax bills.

    St. Charles → · O’Fallon → · St. Peters →

  • North County

    Some of the lowest prices in St. Louis County, mostly houses from the 1950s to the 1970s. Florissant’s 2026 median was $180,000 across 167 sales; Bridgeton’s was $316,000.

    North County guide → · Florissant →

  • Jefferson County

    South of the Meramec River, with more land and the region’s newest resale houses. 2026 medians ran from $222,000 in De Soto to $469,900 on the Jefferson County side of Eureka.

    Jefferson County guide → · Imperial → · Arnold →
