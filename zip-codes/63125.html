ZIP Code 63125

Lemay and Mehlville. South County's most accessible price point — $152K median in Lemay, $218K median in Mehlville. Single school district (Mehlville R-3). Jefferson Barracks Park access. The mistake is assuming all 63125 inventory is identical when Lemay and Mehlville serve different buyer profiles.

What's in 63125

Two Neighborhoods, One ZIP Code

ZIP code 63125 covers Lemay and Mehlville. Both fall inside the Mehlville R-3 school district, so there's no district split to navigate. The price difference between the two neighborhoods is significant. Lemay's median owner-occupied value is $152K. Mehlville's median is $218K. That's a $66K gap inside the same ZIP code.

Lemay is South County's most affordable entry point. It sits along the Mississippi River side of the county with Jefferson Barracks Park as the signature anchor. Housing stock is mature — mostly 1950s-1970s ranches and split-levels. The value proposition is access to Mehlville schools at a payment level below what most Mehlville core buyers are facing.

Mehlville proper is the district's namesake community. It's more centrally located in South County, and the housing stock runs slightly newer and slightly higher in price. When buyers say they want "Mehlville schools," they often mean the core Mehlville area, not Lemay. The ZIP code doesn't make that distinction clear.

School District

Mehlville R-3 Only

ZIP code 63125 is entirely within the Mehlville School District. There's no district split, no overlap with Affton or Lindbergh. If you buy in 63125, you're in Mehlville R-3. That simplifies the school question compared to other South County ZIPs, but it doesn't mean all properties have identical school assignments. Elementary school boundaries still vary by exact address.

Mehlville R-3 is a large district covering much of South County. It's stable and well-regarded, but it doesn't generate the same buyer urgency as Lindbergh. That difference shows up in pricing. A home in Mehlville R-3 will typically cost less than a comparable home in Lindbergh, all else equal.

Typical Price Range

$152K to $270K Depending on Neighborhood

Lemay's median owner-occupied home value is $152K. Mehlville's median is $218K. The actual spread of inventory runs from $140K fixer-uppers in Lemay to $270K move-in ready homes in core Mehlville. Most buyers in 63125 are looking at $190K-$250K.

Lemay Entry Tier · $140K–$200K

South County's most accessible price point. Expect older housing stock, deferred maintenance, and inspection issues. This is the tier for FHA/VA buyers who need payment over everything else.

Mehlville Core · $200K–$270K

Slightly newer inventory, more turnkey options, but still mature housing stock. Expect 1960s-1980s construction, updated systems in the higher range.

Common Home Styles

Ranches, Basements, Mature Stock

The housing stock in 63125 is mature. Most homes were built between 1950 and 1980. Ranches dominate (60%+ of inventory), followed by split-levels and bi-levels. Basements are standard — most are finished or partially finished. Lot sizes run 0.15 to 0.25 acres. You're looking at single-family detached homes on tight lots, not sprawling properties.

Construction Era

1950-1980 is the primary building period. That means you're dealing with 45-75 year old systems in most cases. HVAC, electrical panels, plumbing, and roofing will need attention sooner rather than later.

Buyer Warnings

The 63125 Inspection Checklist

A $190K ranch in Lemay will have $10K-$20K in deferred maintenance. That's the market. Here's what buyers actually find during inspections in this ZIP code:

Federal Pacific Electrical Panels

Common in 1950-1980 construction. Known fire hazard. Insurance companies flag them. Replacement cost: $2K-$4K. Some lenders won't close until replaced.

Read: Federal Pacific Panels & VA Loans →
Cast Iron Drain Lines

Standard in pre-1970 construction. They rust from the inside out. Replacement cost: $8K-$15K. Finished basements make access harder and replacement more expensive.

Galvanized Supply Lines

If the home still has galvanized water supply pipes, they're corroded. Low water pressure is the symptom. Replacement cost: $5K-$10K.

Slab Foundations

Some homes in 63125 sit on slab foundations instead of basements. That means no drain line access without breaking concrete. If you have cast iron drains and a slab foundation, replacement gets expensive fast.

Old HVAC Systems

If the furnace or A/C is 15+ years old, budget for replacement. Cost: $6K-$10K for both. Sellers rarely replace HVAC before listing.

Roof Condition

FHA and VA loans require roofs to have at least 3 years of remaining life. If the roof is shot, the lender won't close until it's replaced. Know the roof condition before you make an offer.
