What's in 63126

Crestwood and Sappington

ZIP code 63126 covers Crestwood and part of Sappington. In 2026 MLS sales, 22 of the 43 listed “St Louis” as the mailing city, 19 “Crestwood,” 1 “Sappington” and 1 “Sunset Hills.”

Crestwood is incorporated with its own parks system (6 parks, 120 acres). Sappington is unincorporated, with tree cover and quiet streets.

The housing stock is mature: the median 2026 sale was built in 1957 with about 1,675 square feet, and 31 of the 43 sales (72.1%) were ranches.

School District

Lindbergh Schools

All 43 of the 2026 sales in 63126 were in the Lindbergh School District. Next door, 63128 split between Lindbergh and Mehlville R-IX. Verify assigned schools for the exact address before you make an offer.

Lindbergh-area prices run higher than many nearby Mehlville R-IX areas, but the houses differ too. Compare the same type of house before deciding what the district line is worth to you.

Market Speed

How Fast 63126 Homes Sold—and for How Much

The median 63126 sale spent 7 days on market. 22 of the 43 sales (51.2%) took a week or less. But 9 (20.9%) took 30 days or more, and the longest took 100 days.

Against the seller’s original list price, 25 sales (58.1%) closed above it, 4 (9.3%) at it and 14 (32.6%) below it. The median sale was 102.70% of the original asking price.

Common Home Styles

Mostly 1950s–60s Ranches

The median 2026 sale in 63126 was built in 1957 and had about 1,675 square feet. By decade: 4 sales were built before 1950, 21 in the 1950s, 12 in the 1960s and 6 in 1970 or later.

Ranches dominate: 31 of the 43 sales (72.1%) listed a ranch style, including ranch combinations. Split-level homes were rare: 2 sales.

Construction Era

33 of the 43 sales (76.7%) were built in the 1950s or 1960s, so most houses here are 55 to 75 years old. HVAC, roofing, plumbing and electrical panels will need attention if they haven’t been replaced already.
