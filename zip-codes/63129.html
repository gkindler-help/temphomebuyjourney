ZIP Code 63129

What's in 63129

Three Neighborhoods, One District

ZIP code 63129 covers Oakville, parts of Mehlville, and parts of Green Park. All three fall inside the Mehlville R-3 school district. No district split to navigate. The price difference between neighborhoods is real but less dramatic than in other South County ZIPs.

Oakville is the flagship. It's the largest community in the ZIP, with a median owner-occupied value of $319K. That makes it the highest-priced neighborhood in the Mehlville district footprint. Oakville runs along the Mississippi River with Cliff Cave Park and Bee Tree Park as anchors. Housing stock is newer — mostly 1980s-1990s construction with some 2000s infill.

Mehlville proper (the parts that fall in 63129) runs around $218K median. Green Park sits in the middle at around $250K-$280K. The common thread is that all three neighborhoods offer less system risk than 63123 or 63125 because the construction era skews 10-20 years newer.

School District

Mehlville R-3 Only

ZIP code 63129 is entirely within the Mehlville School District. No split with Affton or Lindbergh. If you buy in 63129, you're in Mehlville R-3. Elementary school boundaries still vary by exact address, so verify assigned schools before making an offer.

Typical Price Range

$220K to $380K Depending on Neighborhood

Mehlville core in 63129 runs $218K median. Green Park sits around $250K-$280K. Oakville's median is $319K. The actual spread runs from $200K older homes in Mehlville to $380K+ updated homes in Oakville. Most buyers in 63129 are looking at $250K-$340K.

Entry Tier · $200K–$260K

Older stock in Mehlville core, some Green Park properties. Still mature housing, but less deferred maintenance than 63125.

Mid Tier · $260K–$340K

Oakville ranches and bi-levels, 1980s-1990s construction. Updated kitchens, newer systems. This is the sweet spot for buyers who want to avoid major repairs in the first 5 years.

Upper Tier · $340K–$380K+

Larger Oakville homes, 2000s construction, or fully renovated 1990s properties. This tier competes with Concord and Crestwood on price.

Common Home Styles

1980s-1990s Ranches and Bi-Levels

The housing stock in 63129 skews newer than 63123 or 63125. You're looking at 1980s-1990s construction in Oakville, with some 1970s stock in Mehlville core. Ranches are still common, but bi-levels and split-levels show up more frequently here. Basements are standard. Lot sizes run 0.2 to 0.3 acres.

Construction Era

1980-2000 is the primary building period in Oakville. That means you're dealing with 25-45 year old systems instead of 50-70 year old systems. That difference matters for HVAC, roofing, and plumbing.

Buyer Warnings

What to Watch For in 63129

The system risk in 63129 is lower than 63123 or 63125 because the housing stock is newer. Federal Pacific panels are less common. Cast iron drains are less common. But you still need to check for deferred maintenance.

HVAC Age

If the furnace or A/C is 15+ years old, it's near end-of-life. Budget $6K-$10K for replacement. In 1980s-1990s homes, the original HVAC is already past its 20-25 year lifespan.

Roof Condition

Roofs last 20-25 years. If the home was built in 1990 and the roof hasn't been replaced, it's due. Budget $8K-$15K depending on size.

Water Heaters

Water heaters last 10-12 years. If it's older than that, it's a ticking time bomb. Replacement cost: $1K-$2K.

Deferred Exterior Maintenance

Siding, gutters, and downspouts. If the seller hasn't maintained them, you're looking at $3K-$8K to catch up.
