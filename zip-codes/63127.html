What's in 63127

Mostly Sunset Hills

ZIP code 63127 is mostly Sunset Hills. Of the 21 sales in 2026, 10 listed “Sunset Hills” as the mailing city and 11 listed “St Louis.”

The 2026 median was $725,000—about double 63129 ($370,000) and 63126 ($365,000), and three times 63125 ($242,000). With only 21 sales, treat 63127 figures as a rough guide.

The signature anchor is Laumeier Sculpture Park — 105 acres of outdoor art and walking trails. The city's land area is 8.98 square miles with only 9,198 residents (2020 Census). That's 1,024 people per square mile. Compare that to Affton's 4,000+ per square mile. The lower density is intentional and protected.

2026 Home Prices

What 63127 Homes Sold For in 2026

21 single-family homes closed in 63127 from January 1 to October 5, 2026. The median sale price was $725,000. The middle half sold between $520,000 and $970,000, and the full range ran from $162,500 to $2,150,000. The median price per square foot was $248.

With only 21 sales, treat these figures as a rough guide. A few sales in either direction can move the median noticeably.

Market Speed

How Fast 63127 Homes Sold—and for How Much

The median 63127 sale spent 5 days on market. 13 of the 21 sales (61.9%) took a week or less. But 3 (14.3%) took 30 days or more, and the longest took 69 days.

Against the seller’s original list price, 14 sales (66.7%) closed above it, 1 (4.8%) at it and 6 (28.6%) below it. The median sale was 105.53% of the original asking price.

Common Home Styles

Larger Homes, Wide Range

The housing stock in 63127 runs larger than the rest of South County. The median 2026 sale had about 2,620 square feet and was built in 1978.

No single era dominates: 8 of the 21 sales were built before 1960, 7 from 1970 to 1989 and 5 in 2000 or later. 8 of the 21 (38.1%) listed a ranch style.

Construction Era

With homes from the 1950s through the 2000s, system age varies widely here. Check HVAC age, roof condition and water heaters on every house.

Best-Fit Buyer

Who This ZIP Code Actually Fits

Good Fit

Buyers with budgets around $520,000–$970,000—the middle half of 2026 sales in 63127—who want lower density and larger homes without moving to West County.

Good Fit

Buyers who want Laumeier Sculpture Park, Watson Trail and Gempp Nature Area nearby and are comparing Sunset Hills with Kirkwood or Webster Groves on price.

Good Fit

Buyers downsizing from West County who want to compare Sunset Hills on price.

Not a Fit

Buyers capped at $400,000: only 5 of the 21 sales closed at that price or less. Buyers who assume Sunset Hills gets them Clayton or Central West End urban access.
