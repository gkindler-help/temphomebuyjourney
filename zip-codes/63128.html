ZIP Code 63128

Concord and Sappington. School district split: Lindbergh and Mehlville R-3. Median value $298K-$304K. This is where Lindbergh schools become accessible in South County without stepping up to Crestwood or Sunset Hills pricing.

What's in 63128

Two Neighborhoods, Two Districts

ZIP code 63128 covers Concord and Sappington. Both neighborhoods straddle the Lindbergh and Mehlville school district boundary. That district split is the defining characteristic of this ZIP. Two houses on the same street can be in different districts. Zillow doesn't make that clear.

Concord's median owner-occupied value is $304K. Sappington's median is $298K. They're nearly identical on price. The difference is school district assignment. If you want Lindbergh schools and can afford $280K+, 63128 is where that option opens up in South County without paying Crestwood or Sunset Hills premiums.

The housing stock is mature but well-maintained. Mostly 1970s-1980s construction. Ranches and split-levels dominate. Lot sizes run 0.2 to 0.3 acres. You're looking at established neighborhoods with tree cover and quiet streets.

School District Split

Lindbergh vs. Mehlville R-3

ZIP code 63128 is split between Lindbergh School District and Mehlville School District. The boundary runs through both Concord and Sappington. Two houses on the same block can be in different districts. You must verify assigned schools by exact property address before making an offer.

District Coverage in 63128 Buyer Impact
Lindbergh Parts of Concord, parts of Sappington This is the district buyers chase. It commands a premium. Homes in Lindbergh typically sell for $20K-$40K more than comparable homes in Mehlville, all else equal.
Mehlville R-3 Parts of Concord, parts of Sappington Solid district, lower pricing. If you want 63128 location but can't justify Lindbergh premium, Mehlville side offers value.

⚠ School district boundaries do not follow ZIP codes. Verify assigned schools by exact property address before making any school-driven decisions.

Typical Price Range

$250K to $420K Depending on District

Mehlville R-3 side runs $250K-$340K. Lindbergh side runs $290K-$420K. The actual spread runs from $250K older homes on the Mehlville side to $420K+ updated homes on the Lindbergh side. Most buyers in 63128 are looking at $280K-$380K.

Mehlville Side · $250K–$340K

1970s-1980s ranches and split-levels. Updated systems in the higher range. Value play for buyers who want 63128 location without Lindbergh premium.

Lindbergh Side · $290K–$420K

Same housing stock, $30K-$50K higher pricing. The premium buys Lindbergh schools. If that matters to you, 63128 is where you get Lindbergh access without Crestwood or Sunset Hills pricing.
