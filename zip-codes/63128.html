What's in 63128

Two Neighborhoods, Two Districts

ZIP code 63128 covers Concord and Sappington. Both straddle the Lindbergh and Mehlville R-IX district boundary. That split is the defining feature of this ZIP: two houses on the same street can be in different districts, and Zillow doesn’t make that clear.

In 2026, the 57 Mehlville R-IX sales had a $394,500 median and the 37 Lindbergh sales a $425,900 median. But the houses differed: the median Mehlville R-IX-side sale was built in 1977 with about 2,250 sq ft; the median Lindbergh-side sale was built in 1965 with about 2,540 sq ft.

Most of the stock is 1960s–70s: 56 of the 94 sales (59.6%). Half the sales (47 of 94) listed a ranch style, including ranch combinations; split-level homes were 8 (8.5%).

School District Split

Lindbergh vs. Mehlville R-IX

ZIP code 63128 is split between the Lindbergh and Mehlville R-IX school districts, and the boundary runs through both Concord and Sappington. In 2026 closed sales, 57 were in Mehlville R-IX and 37 in Lindbergh. Verify assigned schools for the exact property address before making an offer.

This is a housing comparison, not a school comparison. The houses differ by district, so compare the same type of house before deciding what a district line is worth to you.

Market Speed

How Fast 63128 Homes Sold—and for How Much

The median 63128 sale spent 5.5 days on market. 55 of the 94 sales (58.5%) took a week or less. But 25 (26.6%) took 30 days or more, and the longest took 208 days.

Against the seller’s original list price, 53 sales (56.4%) closed above it, 10 (10.6%) at it and 31 (33.0%) below it. The median sale was 101.25% of the original asking price.
