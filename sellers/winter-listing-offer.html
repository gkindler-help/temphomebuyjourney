Maybe you’re making payments on an empty house. Maybe the listing already expired. Maybe you’re relocating, settling an estate, carrying two properties, dealing with repairs you don’t want to make, facing a deadline, or simply ready to be done.

I’m Not Going to Tell You to Fix Everything.

When you need to sell, spending another $15,000 getting a house “market ready” isn’t automatically the right answer. I separate repairs into three buckets.

Fix It
Likely to materially affect marketability, financing, safety, or the transaction.
Price for It
Buyers will notice it, but fixing it may not return enough to justify the money or time.
Leave It Alone
Unlikely to meaningfully change the outcome.

The goal isn’t to make your house perfect. It’s to decide where another dollar actually helps you sell it.

Already Tried to Sell?

Don’t relist the same house with a new sign and expect a different result.

An expired or withdrawn listing leaves evidence. The pattern of showings, offers, feedback, price changes, days on market, and where buyers stopped responding can tell us a lot about what happened.

Did you get enough showings?
Did buyers tour but not make offers?
Did you receive offers but couldn’t reach terms?
Did a deal go under contract and then fall apart?
Did activity noticeably drop after the first couple weeks?

These five questions don’t tell the whole story. But the full diagnostic can.
