Selling a Vacant House in St. Louis: Cash Offer or Listing?

By George Kindler - Licensed Missouri REALTOR - 13 Years Experience

Vacant homes attract unsolicited cash offers.

Do the math first: Monthly carrying cost times expected months to close is the number to compare against the investor discount.

Real carrying costs to calculate

Mortgage, taxes, insurance, utilities, lawn care, security, vandalism risk.

City of St. Louis vacancy registration

The City of St. Louis has vacant building registration and code enforcement requirements. Failure to register can create violations and fines.

Insurance complications

Standard homeowner policies may limit or exclude claims after 30-60 days of vacancy.
