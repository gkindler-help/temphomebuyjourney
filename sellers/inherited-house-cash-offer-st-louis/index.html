Inherited a House in St. Louis? Read This Before Taking a Cash Offer

By George Kindler - Licensed Missouri REALTOR - 13 Years Experience

An inherited house can attract cash buyers quickly.

Authority first: Before discussing price, confirm who has legal authority to sell.

Why inherited homes attract investors

In South County and Oakville, many inherited homes have solid locations but dated systems.

Missouri probate basics

If the property is still in probate, the personal representative may need authority from the court or the estate documents.

Do not ignore tax basis

Heirs often receive a stepped-up cost basis to fair market value at the date of death.

Compare cash with realistic alternatives

Use Investor Math and the Cash Offer Decoder. If vacant, read Vacant House.

Questions heirs should ask

Use Questions to Ask and Proof of Funds.

The bottom line

Confirm authority, understand probate, get tax guidance, compare the market, then decide.
