What is North County St. Louis?

North County is the northern part of St. Louis County. This guide covers 15 communities with their own pages, from Maryland Heights, Bridgeton and St. Ann in the west to Bellefontaine Neighbors and Spanish Lake in the east and Florissant and Black Jack in the north, plus 32 small towns. Together they had 762 single-family sales in 2026.

How much does a house cost in North County?

The 2026 median sale price across these communities was $151,500. Community medians ran from $80,000 in Moline Acres to $316,000 in Bridgeton, and the 32 small towns together had an $87,500 median.

What are the most affordable areas in North County?

By 2026 median sale price: Moline Acres ($80,000); Jennings ($88,000); Bellefontaine Neighbors ($94,500); Berkeley ($101,000).

Can I buy a house in North County for $150,000?

Yes. 377 of 762 sales in 2026 (49.5%) closed at $150,000 or less. In Florissant it was 46 of 167; in Hazelwood, 11 of 63.

What does $200,000 buy in North County?

Most of the market. 527 of 762 sales in 2026 (69.2%) closed at $200,000 or less, including every sale in Bellefontaine Neighbors, Berkeley, Dellwood and Moline Acres. In Black Jack it was 3 of 16, and in Bridgeton 2 of 22.

In 2026 St. Louis County sales, North County districts gave the most square footage at four of the five budgets compared. Among homes that closed for $250,001 to $300,000, Ferguson-Florissant led every St. Louis County district at a median 2,051 square feet; at $350,000 and $400,000, Hazelwood led.

How fast do homes sell in North County?

The median was 20 days on market in 2026, but it varied by community: Maryland Heights 7 days, Bridgeton 7.5 days, Black Jack 8.5 days, against Dellwood 54, St. John 39, Moline Acres 38.

Do North County homes sell over asking price?

Some do. 236 of 762 sales in 2026 (31.0%) closed above the original list price. Among communities with at least 20 sales, Maryland Heights (37 of 72) and Hazelwood (29 of 63) had the highest shares. With most homes closing at or under list, buyers rarely need to give up a contingency to win.

How old are most homes in North County?

The median year built across 2026 sales was 1956. St. John (1947) and St. Ann (1950) had the oldest homes on median; Black Jack (1973) and Bridgeton (1970) the newest.

Which school districts serve North County?

Eight, in 2026 sales across these communities: Ferguson-Florissant (216), Hazelwood (188), Pattonville (82), Riverview Gardens (79), Ritenour (71), Normandy Schools Collaborative (67), Jennings (37), Parkway (22). District lines don’t follow city lines: Florissant split between Ferguson-Florissant (90) and Hazelwood (77), and Dellwood crossed three districts.

How much are property taxes in North County?

The median annual property tax on 2026 sales across these communities was $2,148, according to MLS sales records. By school district it ran from $1,453 in the Normandy Schools Collaborative to $3,532 in Parkway. The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue before you make an offer.

See taxes by district →

Are North County towns incorporated?

Almost all of them. Of the 15 communities in this guide, 14 are incorporated cities; Spanish Lake is an unincorporated Census-designated place with no city government. Each city sets its own rules for occupancy permits and inspections when a house changes hands, so ask the city for the exact address before you close.

What are North County’s small towns?

32 small towns, from Bel-Nor to Wellston, many of them under a square mile. Together they had 111 sales in 2026 at an $87,500 median, too few in most towns for a page of their own, so they share one guide.

What ZIP codes are in North County?

This guide’s communities used 16 ZIP codes in 2026 sales. ZIP codes follow mailing addresses, not city limits or school districts: 63031 covered most Florissant sales and part of Hazelwood, and 63135 covered Ferguson and part of Dellwood.

What are the main roads in North County?

I-270 crosses North County east to west. I-70 runs past Lambert Airport toward the city. U.S. 67 (Lindbergh Boulevard) runs through Florissant and Hazelwood, and Route 367 heads north from I-270 past Spanish Lake.
