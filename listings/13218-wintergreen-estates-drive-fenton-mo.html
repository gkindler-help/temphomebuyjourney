About This Home
Fully Renovated in Lindbergh Schools — Fenton, MO

13218 Wintergreen Estates Drive is a 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom multi-split in Fenton's Wintergreen Estates subdivision — tucked on a cul-de-sac lot with mature trees and a fully fenced backyard. Built in 1997 and completely refreshed for its next owner.

The sellers went through everything: new LVP flooring throughout, new paint, new lighting, new fixtures, new plumbing, new stainless appliances, and a new roof. The kitchen features an island wrapped in granite, white shaker cabinets, and stainless steel appliances in an open-concept layout with vaulted ceilings and a fireplace. The primary suite includes a large private bath with both soaking tub and shower, and double vanity with new fixtures.

The finished lower level adds a full third bathroom — ideal for guests, a home office, or additional entertaining space. Lindbergh Schools serve this address — Concord Elementary, Sperreng Middle, and Lindbergh Senior High.
