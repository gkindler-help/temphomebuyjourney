About This Home
Updated Ranch on a Corner Lot — Maryland Heights, MO

11919 Holly Brook Drive is a 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath ranch in the Brookside subdivision of Maryland Heights, set on a level corner lot of roughly a quarter acre with a large fenced backyard. Built in 1962 and thoughtfully updated throughout for its next owner.

Inside you'll find refinished hardwood floors and a completely updated kitchen with new cabinets, granite countertops, new flooring, and stainless steel appliances. Down the hall are three bedrooms and a tastefully updated full bathroom. The partially finished lower level adds around 544 square feet of flexible space with a fireplace — ideal as a family room, home office, or entertaining area — plus a half bath.

Out back, a covered patio overlooks a spacious yard with room for gardening, get-togethers, or pets, and two included sheds keep the one-car attached garage free for parking. Pattonville R-III Schools serve this address — Parkwood Elementary, Pattonville Heights Middle, and Pattonville Senior High.
