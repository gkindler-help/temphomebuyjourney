About This Home
Split Foyer With a Finished Walk-Out Lower Level — Oakville, MO

7205 Meramar Drive is a 4-bedroom, 3-full-bath split foyer in the Emerald Hill subdivision of Oakville, with 2,180 square feet of living space: 1,180 on the upper level and about 1,000 more in the partially finished, walk-out lower level. Built in 1981 on a quarter-acre lot (0.251 acre) with mature landscaping.

The main level has vaulted ceilings, a dining room, and an eat-in kitchen with white cabinets, stainless steel appliances and a door out to the back. Three bedrooms and two full baths are on this level. Downstairs, the lower-level family room has a brick fireplace and a sliding door that walks out to the back yard, with the fourth bedroom and third full bath on the same level.

Several big-ticket items have been done within the last five years: a new roof (architectural shingle), siding, deck, HVAC system and water heater, plus a new garage service door. That’s fewer major expenses to plan for in your first years of owning it. Outside, there’s a back yard, a deck and a 2-car attached garage.
