STL Home Journey is a St. Louis real estate education resource created by George Kindler, a licensed Missouri real estate agent with 13 years of experience and 250+ transactions across the metro. The site helps buyers and sellers understand pricing, neighborhoods, school districts, inspections, affordability, contracts, selling strategy, and the specific decisions that determine what they ultimately pay or net. Coverage focuses on South County, South City, West County, Jefferson County, St. Charles, and the surrounding St. Louis market, with original local analysis, market data, case studies from actual transactions, and interactive decision tools.
