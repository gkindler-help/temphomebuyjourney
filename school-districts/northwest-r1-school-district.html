How much do houses cost in the Northwest R-I School District?

In 152 sales that closed January 6–October 2, 2026, the median sale price was $301,000, according to MLS sales records. The middle half sold between $250,000 and $385,625, and the full range ran from $70,000 to $1,091,000.

Methodology

How This Northwest R-I Analysis Was Built

This page uses 152 closed single-family sales from the STL Home Journey 2026 Jefferson County sales dataset (January 6–October 2, 2026): a countywide MLS export of vacant or owner-occupied single-family resales, excluding new construction and tenant-occupied properties. School district comes from the high school listed in MLS. The same records feed the Jefferson County school district housing analysis.

  • Historical closed sales are not current inventory.
  • Days on market uses CDOM.
  • Sale ÷ original ask compares the close price with the original list price.
  • Ranch share counts any MLS style that includes “Ranch.”
  • Property taxes are each seller’s most recent annual bill as listed in MLS.

Property taxes · 2026What are property taxes in the Northwest R-I School District?

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Northwest R-I School District in 2026 was $2,227, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $1,412 to $3,573, across 151 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.

In Northwest R-I, the median annual property tax was $1,130 for homes with 1–2 bedrooms, $1,897 with 3, $3,039 with 4 and $4,274 with 5 or more, based on 2026 MLS sales records.
