Methodology

How This Windsor C-1 Analysis Was Built

This page uses 58 closed single-family sales from the STL Home Journey 2026 Jefferson County sales dataset (January 12–September 28, 2026): a countywide MLS export of vacant or owner-occupied single-family resales, excluding new construction and tenant-occupied properties. School district comes from the high school listed in MLS. The same records feed the Jefferson County school district housing analysis.

  • Historical closed sales are not current inventory.
  • Days on market uses CDOM.
  • Sale ÷ original ask compares the close price with the original list price.
  • Ranch share counts any MLS style that includes “Ranch.”
  • Property taxes are each seller’s most recent annual bill as listed in MLS.

Property taxes · 2026What are property taxes in the Windsor C-1 School District?

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Windsor C-1 School District in 2026 was $2,284, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $1,913 to $3,519, across 58 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.
