How This Hancock Place Analysis Was Built

Property taxes · 2026What are property taxes in the Hancock Place School District?

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Hancock Place School District in 2026 was $1,994, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $1,801 to $2,428, across 26 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.
