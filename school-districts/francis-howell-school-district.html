Francis Howell School District: What Home Buyers Need to Know

Francis Howell School District, officially Francis Howell R-III, is one of the larger school districts in St. Charles County. It serves about 17,000 students across a wide western-metro footprint that includes parts of St. Charles, St. Peters, Cottleville, O'Fallon, Weldon Spring and surrounding areas of St. Charles County.

For buyers, Francis Howell is a district where the exact address matters. The district has three comprehensive high schools — Francis Howell High, Francis Howell North and Francis Howell Central — so confirming that a home is inside Francis Howell is only the first step. You also need to verify which high school serves that property.

The district is currently fully accredited by Missouri DESE and has several strong institutional indicators, including multiple National Blue Ribbon Schools of Excellence, National Schools of Character and an AA+ financial rating.

Those are useful facts, but I still wouldn't choose a home from the district name alone. I would look at the specific high school assignment, the individual schools tied to the property, the house itself and what your budget buys in St. Charles County compared with other parts of the metro.

Who it's forWho is Francis Howell School District a good fit for?

Francis Howell tends to come into the conversation for buyers who are looking farther west into St. Charles County and want access to a larger suburban school system with a broad range of neighborhoods and housing options.

One of the biggest real estate differences here is the housing stock. Compared with many inner-ring St. Louis County communities, buyers in Francis Howell will often encounter more recently built subdivisions and a larger supply of homes from newer development periods. Depending on the specific community, that can mean different floor plans, larger garages, newer mechanical systems or neighborhood layouts that simply weren't built in the same way in older parts of the metro.

That doesn't automatically make newer housing better. It just changes what you're comparing. Some buyers would rather have a newer two-story in St. Charles County. Someone else may prefer an older home closer to the urban core, even if the floor plan or lot looks different. Commute and purchase price can also change substantially as you move farther west.

I think Francis Howell makes the most sense to evaluate when you're willing to compare the full package instead of starting with the assumption that one school district should decide everything else.

What you're paying forWhat are you paying for when you buy in Francis Howell?

Francis Howell is one of those districts where the school name and the housing market can become tied together in a buyer's mind. I think it's better to separate them and look at what you're actually paying for. Start with the property.

How much house are you getting? How old is it? What does the lot look like? Does it have the garage, basement, bedrooms and layout you need? How much of your monthly payment is going toward principal and interest versus taxes and insurance? Then compare that with the alternatives.

A home in St. Peters or Cottleville may give you a newer subdivision or a particular floor plan that is harder to find in an older St. Louis County community. At the same time, buying farther west may change your commute, your proximity to family or how much time you spend driving to the places you use regularly. That tradeoff matters.

I wouldn't stretch a budget simply because a home is inside a district with strong recognition. If you're buying too much house or creating a payment that makes the rest of your finances uncomfortable, the school district doesn't erase that. On the other hand, if Francis Howell's schools, housing stock and location line up with what you want and the payment works, then paying more than you would in another part of the metro may make sense for you. This is why I like starting with realistic buying power. Once you know the payment range you're comfortable with, it's much easier to compare Francis Howell with another St. Charles County district or a St. Louis County option without losing sight of the actual cost of the house.

High schoolsThe three Francis Howell high schools

Francis Howell School District has three comprehensive high schools that are most relevant to home buyers: Francis Howell High School, Francis Howell North High School and Francis Howell Central High School.

  • Francis Howell High School is in the St. Charles area. It dates back to 1881, serves roughly 1,843 students and has received national recognition, including National Blue Ribbon School status.
  • Francis Howell North High School is in St. Peters and began operating as a high school in 1986. It serves roughly 1,716 students.
  • Francis Howell Central High School is in Cottleville, opened in 1997 and serves roughly 1,750 students. Its attendance area includes a significant portion of the St. Peters area.

The district also operates non-traditional or alternative options, including Francis Howell Union and Westwood Trail Academy, but the three comprehensive high schools above are generally the ones buyers are comparing when evaluating a home's traditional high-school assignment.

Because this is a multi-high-school district, I would not try to determine the school assignment from the city name alone. A St. Peters address does not automatically tell you whether a student attends Francis Howell North or Francis Howell Central. The same general issue applies around other municipal boundaries throughout the district.

Use Francis Howell's official school or boundary locator for the exact address you're considering. If the high-school assignment is important enough that it would change your decision about the house, verify it directly with the district before you rely on it. I also wouldn't treat the school information shown on Zillow, Realtor.com, the MLS or another real estate platform as the final authority. Those tools are useful during the search, but official district information is the better source for confirming current assignments.

CommunitiesWhat communities are in Francis Howell School District?

Francis Howell covers a large portion of central and southern St. Charles County and serves parts of St. Charles, St. Peters, Cottleville, O'Fallon, Weldon Spring and surrounding areas.

The district headquarters is in O'Fallon, but that does not mean all O'Fallon addresses are automatically inside Francis Howell. This is the same issue buyers run into throughout the St. Louis metro: municipal boundaries, mailing addresses, ZIP codes and school-district boundaries don't always match. That's why I use city names as general location references, not as proof of school assignment.

If Francis Howell is an important part of your search, my process would be:

  • Confirm that the exact property is inside Francis Howell School District.
  • Use the district's official locator to determine the assigned elementary, middle and high schools.
  • Verify whether the home feeds Francis Howell High, Francis Howell North or Francis Howell Central.
  • Review the latest Missouri DESE information for the district and individual schools.
  • Use GreatSchools as an additional research tool, not the only source.
  • Confirm any specific programs directly with the district if they matter to your purchase.

That matters particularly in St. Peters and other communities where district and high-school boundaries can be less obvious from the mailing address.
