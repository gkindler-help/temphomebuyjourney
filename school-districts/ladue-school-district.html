Who it's forBuyer fit for Ladue School District

Ladue is for buyers who want elite schools, larger homes, established wealth, and one of the strongest resale names in the St. Louis market. It fits high-budget families, relocation buyers, executives, and buyers who value privacy, lot size, and long-term reputation.

What you're paying forValue breakdown

You are paying for school reputation, land, prestige, and access to some of the most established suburbs in the region. Ladue buyers are often paying for more than the house; they are paying for the street, the lot, the district, and the long-term confidence of owning in a market with durable demand. Compared with Clayton, Ladue can offer more land and privacy, though often with a more car-dependent lifestyle. The premium is real, and condition updates can still be expensive even after paying the entry price.

NeighborhoodsWhere buyers look inside this district

Ladue is the flagship name, with estate-style homes, large lots, and buyers who expect privacy, schools, and prestige. Frontenac brings luxury retail, larger homes, and a high-end suburban feel with strong demand from upper-budget buyers. Huntleigh is ultra-exclusive and low-density, with estate properties and buyers focused on privacy above convenience. Warson Woods feels smaller and more residential, often attracting families who want Ladue schools with a slightly more neighborhood feel. Crestwood portions tied into the Ladue conversation can create confusion, so buyers need to verify boundaries carefully before assuming school access. Olivette offers a more central and sometimes more attainable path into the district, but prices still reflect the school premium.

From the fieldWhat I've seen on the ground

George Kindler — Licensed MO Agent · The Closing Pros LLC

I have seen buyers walk into Ladue thinking the budget was huge, then realize the market still expects them to compromise somewhere. Maybe the kitchen is dated, maybe the lot is the value, or maybe the house needs work even at a very high price. In Ladue, the name gets you in the door, but the street, lot, and condition still decide whether the price makes sense.

Honest takeWhat to know before you decide

Ladue is elite, expensive, and not trying to be affordable. It does schools, prestige, land, and long-term resale confidence extremely well. It falls short for buyers who want walkability, newer finishes at every price point, or a simple value calculation. If you want old-money St. Louis, larger lots, and one of the safest school-district names in the region, Ladue is the benchmark.

Property taxes · 2026What are property taxes in the Ladue School District?

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Ladue School District in 2026 was $10,055, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $6,900 to $14,047, across 81 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.
