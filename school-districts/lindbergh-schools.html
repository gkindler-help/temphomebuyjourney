Who it's forBuyer fit for Lindbergh Schools

Lindbergh is for buyers who want this district specifically without moving into the most expensive Central Corridor suburbs. It fits buyers who want South County convenience and still want more house for the money than Clayton, Ladue, or Kirkwood.

What you're paying forValue breakdown

You are paying more per square foot than in neighboring districts: about $208 in 2026 sales, compared with about $175 in Mehlville and $192 in Affton. Inventory is thinner, too: 146 Lindbergh sales in 2026 versus 285 in Mehlville. Compared with West County districts, Lindbergh can still feel like value if you are willing to accept older homes, smaller lots, or dated interiors.

NeighborhoodsWhere buyers look inside this district

Sunset Hills gives buyers larger homes, higher price points, and a more established suburban feel with quick access to highways and shopping. Crestwood is one of the best value pockets, with smaller homes, strong demand, and buyers who want Lindbergh without luxury pricing. Affton portions inside Lindbergh can be a smart play for buyers who care more about district access than a flashy subdivision name. Green Park is compact and convenient, with practical homes and buyers who like being close to South County amenities. Concord offers a central South County location with steady demand, especially when the home has good layout and condition. Sappington feels established, with a mix of ranches, split-levels, and buyers looking for long-term stability.

Honest takeWhat to know before you decide

The honest version is that Lindbergh is not cheap anymore, but it still offers one of the strongest value-to-school combinations in the St. Louis area. Buyers love it because they can get elite school perception without always paying elite West County prices. The tradeoff is older housing stock and intense competition when a clean, well-priced home hits the market. If schools, resale, and South County convenience are your priorities, Lindbergh is one of the safest bets.

Property taxes · 2026What are property taxes in the Lindbergh Schools?

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Lindbergh Schools in 2026 was $4,048, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $3,122 to $5,445, across 146 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.
