How much do houses cost in the Bayless School District?

In 31 sales that closed January 8–September 30, 2026, the median sale price was $254,799, according to MLS sales records. The middle half sold between $214,112 and $265,000, and the full range ran from $38,500 to $430,000.

Methodology

How This Bayless Analysis Was Built

This page uses 31 closed single-family sales from the STL Home Journey 2026 St. Louis County sales dataset (January 8–September 30, 2026), built from district-specific MARIS exports. The same records feed the St. Louis County school district housing analysis.

  • Days on market uses CDOM.
  • Sale ÷ original ask compares the close price with the original list price.
  • Ranch share counts any MLS style that includes “Ranch.”
  • Property taxes are each seller’s most recent annual bill as listed in MLS.

Property taxes · 2026What are property taxes in the Bayless School District?

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Bayless School District in 2026 was $2,610, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $2,315 to $2,916, across 31 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.
