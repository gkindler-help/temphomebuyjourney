Riverview Gardens School District: What Home Buyers Need to Know

Last updated November 5, 2026

Riverview Gardens School District is a smaller North St. Louis County district serving roughly 5,200 students across several municipalities in the northern part of the county.

For a home buyer, there are two things I'd understand early in the research process. First, Riverview Gardens is a single-high-school district. Once you've confirmed that a property is actually inside the district, Riverview Gardens High School is the district's comprehensive high school. You don't have the additional high-school boundary question that comes with larger districts such as Hazelwood or Ferguson-Florissant.

Second, Riverview Gardens School District is currently provisionally accredited by the Missouri Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, or Missouri DESE. Local governance has been restored, and recent performance has brought the district close to full accreditation.

That status needs some context because buyers searching online may find older information saying Riverview Gardens was unaccredited or under state oversight. That's part of the district's history, but it is not the current situation. Today, RGSD is provisionally accredited, locally governed and approaching a potentially important accreditation decision for the 2026-27 school year.

High schoolRiverview Gardens High School and the single-high-school structure

Riverview Gardens High School is the district’s only comprehensive high school. It serves approximately 1,246 students based on 2024-25 information.

From a real estate standpoint, having one comprehensive high school makes the district relatively easy to understand once you've established that a property is inside RGSD. Compare that with a district such as Hazelwood, where buyers have to determine whether the address feeds Hazelwood Central, East or West. In Riverview Gardens, you don't have that second comprehensive-high-school boundary question.

That doesn't mean you should skip address verification. Buyers who care about the younger grades still need to identify the elementary and middle-school assignments associated with a property. More importantly, you should confirm that the property is actually inside Riverview Gardens School District rather than assuming from the municipality or ZIP code. St. Louis County school districts don't always line up cleanly with city boundaries or mailing addresses. If school assignment affects whether you would purchase the home, I recommend verifying the address directly through Riverview Gardens School District or its official school information rather than relying solely on Zillow, Realtor.com, the MLS or another home-search website.

AccreditationRiverview Gardens School District accreditation

As of the 2026–27 school year, Riverview Gardens School District is provisionally accredited and locally governed, and the state is expected to revisit its status. Check Missouri DESE for the current standing before you rely on any article, including this one.

CoverageWhat area does Riverview Gardens School District cover?

Riverview Gardens School District serves several municipalities and communities in northern St. Louis County. Because the district covers portions of a broader North County area, I'd treat the exact property address as the important piece of information rather than the city name or ZIP code printed on the listing.

This comes up constantly in St. Louis County real estate. Municipal boundaries, mailing addresses, ZIP codes and school-district boundaries are separate systems. A buyer can look at two homes relatively close to each other and find that they're assigned to different school districts. If you're specifically searching for homes in Riverview Gardens School District, I'd verify each serious property individually. For a serious property, I'd work through this:

  • Confirm that the exact address is inside Riverview Gardens School District.
  • Verify the currently assigned schools through the district.
  • Confirm Riverview Gardens High as the comprehensive high-school path.
  • Check Missouri DESE for the district's current accreditation classification.
  • Review the newest Annual Performance Report information rather than relying on old performance data.
  • Research the individual schools and programs that matter to your household.

That final accreditation check is especially important here because RGSD is actively approaching a classification decision for the 2026-27 school year.

FAQRiverview Gardens School District: common buyer questions

Is Riverview Gardens School District accredited?

Yes, but Riverview Gardens is currently provisionally accredited, not fully accredited. Local governance has been restored, and the district recently reported finishing one point short of the full-accreditation threshold. Buyers should check Missouri DESE for the latest status because the district is approaching a classification decision for 2026-27.

How many high schools does Riverview Gardens have?

Riverview Gardens School District has one comprehensive high school: Riverview Gardens High School. Because it's a single-high-school district, every address confirmed inside RGSD follows that comprehensive high-school path, although younger students' individual school assignments should still be verified.

What is happening with Riverview Gardens accreditation?

RGSD is currently provisionally accredited and recently came within one point of the full-accreditation threshold. Missouri is expected to use the district's 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26 APR results in determining its classification for 2026-27, so buyers should verify the latest status directly with DESE.

What should buyers check before buying in Riverview Gardens School District?

Confirm that the exact property is inside the district, verify the assigned schools and review the latest Missouri DESE accreditation and performance information. Because RGSD's accreditation status is improving and could change with the upcoming classification decision, I wouldn't rely on older articles or school information attached automatically to a real estate listing.

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records
