Deciding on a house in the Brentwood School District

What should I inspect on a 1940s Brentwood house?

Nearly every Brentwood sale in 2026 (27 of 28) was built before 1960, and the median was built in 1941. On a house that age, a sewer scope ($150 to $300) and a close look at the electrical and the original plumbing come first.

What the inspector checks → · Repair risk by age →

Brentwood homes go over asking. Should I shorten my inspection window to compete?

You can tighten it without dropping it. Fast Brentwood sales closed at a median 104.6% of asking in 2026. On a move-in-ready house, a shorter window or agreeing to raise only major items keeps you competitive and protected. The other ways to compete without going in blind are giving up repair requests, inspecting named items or setting a dollar floor.

Property taxes · 2026What are property taxes in the Brentwood School District?

Updated October 2026 · MLS sales records

The median annual property tax on homes that sold in the Brentwood School District in 2026 was $4,130, according to MLS sales records. The middle half of tax bills ran from $3,431 to $5,568, across 28 sales from January 1 to October 5, 2026.

The bill follows the county’s assessed value and the local tax rates, not the price the house sold for, so two similar homes on the same street can have different bills.
