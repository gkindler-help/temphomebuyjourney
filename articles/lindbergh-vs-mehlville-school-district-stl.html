Lindbergh vs. Mehlville School District: What South County Buyers Need to Know

Both districts serve South St. Louis County. Both are strong performers. But which block you buy on determines which district you're in — and in overlap zones like Concord, the boundary can shift from one street to the next. Here's what buyers need to know before they make an offer.

Why District Assignment Matters More Than Most Buyers Realize

School district is one of the top three criteria buyers cite when searching South County. But most buyers treat it as a neighborhood-level question when it is actually an address-level question. In South St. Louis County, Lindbergh Schools and Mehlville School District serve neighboring — and in some cases overlapping — communities. A home on one side of a street may feed into Lindbergh; the home directly across feeds into Mehlville.

This matters beyond school preference. District assignment is priced into the market. Homes in the Lindbergh footprint command a premium over comparable homes in the Mehlville footprint, sometimes significantly so. In overlap zones, buyers who do not verify before offering can overpay relative to what the district assignment actually supports — or miss that a home they passed on was actually in the district they wanted.

The Concord Problem — South County's Biggest Buyer Confusion Point

Concord is where district confusion causes the most buyer mistakes. It is an unincorporated community that spans zip codes 63128 and 63123, and it sits directly in the overlap zone between Lindbergh and Mehlville.

Mehlville School District serves the majority of Concord. Lindbergh Schools serves Concord Village specifically. These two areas are adjacent. A buyer who searches "Concord MO homes for sale" will see results from both district footprints mixed together — and most listing descriptions do not clearly state which district applies.

Buyers who care about district assignment in Concord must verify every address individually. Not by zip code. Not by community name. By the specific street address, confirmed directly with the relevant district.

How District Assignment Affects What You Pay

District assignment is priced into the South County market, particularly at the Lindbergh end. Homes in established Lindbergh communities — Crestwood, Sappington, Sunset Hills — carry a premium that reflects both the district's reputation and the relative scarcity of Lindbergh-assigned inventory compared to the larger Mehlville footprint.

The median owner-occupied home values tell part of the story: Sunset Hills sits at approximately $523,800, Crestwood at $319,600, Sappington at $297,600. Compare those to Mehlville at $218,400, Concord at $303,600, and Oakville at $318,600 — a market where Mehlville-district homes can reach similar values but across a much larger inventory pool.

In overlap zones, the premium can exist block to block. Two adjacent homes with similar square footage, condition, and lot size can carry different price expectations depending solely on which district they fall in. This is exactly why verifying district assignment before offering — not after — is part of doing this correctly.

How to Verify District Assignment Before You Offer

Here is the only reliable process:

  1. Get the full property address — street number, street name, zip code. Do not rely on a neighborhood name or zip code alone.
  2. Contact the district directly. Call Lindbergh Schools at (314) 729-2480 or Mehlville School District directly with the address. Ask which school the address feeds into at each level — elementary, middle, and high school.
  3. Verify through Missouri DESE. The Missouri Department of Elementary and Secondary Education maintains district boundary data. This is the authoritative source.
  4. Do not use Zillow or Redfin school data. These platforms pull district information from aggregated databases that are frequently wrong in overlap zones. Multiple South County buyers have made offers based on incorrect district information displayed on Zillow.
  5. Have your agent confirm in writing before you waive any contingencies. District assignment is a material fact. If you are buying specifically for the district, that verification should happen before you are under contract.
The Listing Agent Is Not a Reliable Source Either Listing agents sometimes display incorrect district information in MLS — either because they did not verify or because a previous owner told them something inaccurate. Lindbergh vs Mehlville FAQ

Which is better, Lindbergh or Mehlville school district?

Both are top performers. Lindbergh scored 93.5% and Mehlville scored 91.7% on the 2024–2025 Missouri Annual Performance Report — both above the 90% threshold that places a district among the state's top performers. The gap is real but not dramatic. The more important question is which district serves your specific address.

Is Concord in Lindbergh or Mehlville school district?

Both. Mehlville serves most of Concord; Lindbergh serves Concord Village specifically. These areas are adjacent. You must verify the specific address you are considering — zip code and community name are not reliable indicators in Concord.

Does school district affect home price in South County?

Yes — significantly. Lindbergh-assigned homes carry a price premium, particularly in Crestwood, Sappington, and Sunset Hills. In overlap zones the premium can exist block to block. Verify district assignment before offering, not after.

What school district serves Oakville MO?

Oakville is entirely within the Mehlville School District. Students in the Oakville area attend Oakville High School, which is one of two high schools in the Mehlville district. Verify your specific address directly with the district.

How do I verify which school district a South County home is in?

Contact Lindbergh Schools at (314) 729-2480 or the Mehlville School District directly with the specific street address. Do not rely on Zillow, Redfin, or the listing description — these sources are frequently incorrect in South County overlap zones.

What district serves Affton MO?

Affton is served by the Affton School District — a separate district from both Lindbergh and Mehlville. Buyers targeting a Lindbergh or Mehlville address should not assume Affton falls under either.
