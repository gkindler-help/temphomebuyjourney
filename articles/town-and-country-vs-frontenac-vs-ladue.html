Town and Country vs. Frontenac vs. Ladue Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Short answers first, from 2026 closed single-family sales inside each city’s Census boundary: 31 in Town and Country, 11 in Frontenac and 27 in Ladue, January through September. Frontenac’s 11 sales are a small group, so treat its numbers as examples. These are past sales, not what’s for sale today.

Is Ladue more expensive than Town and Country?

By the square foot, much more. Ladue’s 2026 median was $1,430,000 and Town and Country’s $1,348,000, close at the median. But Ladue sold for $387 a square foot and Town and Country $257, a $130 gap, roughly $570,000 on a 4,400-square-foot house. Town and Country’s homes were also newer (built 1982 vs. 1955).

Is Frontenac cheaper than Ladue?

Somewhat, on only 11 sales. Frontenac’s 2026 median was $1,325,000 against Ladue’s $1,430,000, and it sold for $329 a square foot versus $387. All 11 Frontenac sales were in the Ladue School District, the same district as every Ladue sale.

What does $1 million buy in Town and Country, Frontenac or Ladue?

Far more in Town and Country. At $1,000,000 or less, a 2026 budget reached 11 of 31 Town and Country sales (35.5%), 5 of 11 in Frontenac and 3 of 27 in Ladue (11.1%). The Town and Country homes at that budget had a median of 3,873 sq ft; the three in Ladue, 2,162 sq ft.

What about $1.5 million or $2 million?

At $1,500,000 or less: 19 of 31 Town and Country sales (61.3%), 6 of 11 in Frontenac and 15 of 27 in Ladue (55.6%). At $2,000,000 or less: 25 (80.6%), 7 of 11 and 18 (66.7%). Above $1.5 million, Ladue and Town and Country each had 12 sales.

Which has newer homes, Town and Country, Frontenac or Ladue?

Town and Country. Its median 2026 sale was built in 1982, with 9 of 31 (29.0%) built in 1990 or later. Ladue’s median was built in 1955, and 20 of its 27 sales (74.1%) were built before 1970. Frontenac’s median was 1979, but with 11 sales it ranged from older homes to new construction.

Which sells fastest, Town and Country, Frontenac or Ladue?

Frontenac and Ladue. The median days on market (CDOM) was 3 in Frontenac, 7 in Ladue and 14 in Town and Country. 8 of 11 Frontenac sales and 14 of 27 Ladue sales (51.9%) closed above the original list price, versus 11 of 31 in Town and Country (35.5%).

More Town and Country vs. Frontenac vs. Ladue Questions

Are property taxes higher in Ladue, Frontenac or Town and Country?

Highest in Ladue in dollars. The median annual property tax on 2026 sales with a tax record was $13,692 in Ladue (about 0.93% of the sale price), $10,898 in Frontenac (about 1.00%) and $9,308 in Town and Country (about 0.80%), according to MLS sales records. The tax on a listing is the seller’s most recent bill, so confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue.

What does a four-bedroom house cost in Town and Country vs. Ladue?

In 2026 sales, the median home with four or more bedrooms sold for $1,450,000 in Town and Country (27 sales) and $1,800,000 in Ladue (19 sales). The Town and Country houses were larger (5,041 vs. 4,674 sq ft) and newer (built 1985 vs. 1960).

Do sellers take less than asking in Town and Country or Ladue?

More often in Town and Country. 17 of 31 Town and Country sales (54.8%) closed below the original list price, a median 6.0% under. In Ladue, 12 of 27 (44.4%) did, a median 8.1% under. The median Town and Country sale closed at 98.5% of the original list price; Ladue’s at 100.1%.
