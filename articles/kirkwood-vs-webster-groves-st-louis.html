Kirkwood vs. Webster Groves: What 2026 Sales Show

Last updated October 22, 2026

Kirkwood homes sold for more in 2026, a $566,750 median versus $444,500 in Webster Groves, but mostly because Kirkwood’s houses were bigger and newer. Kirkwood vs. Webster Groves Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Short answers first, from 2026 closed single-family sales inside each city’s Census boundary: 80 in Kirkwood (January 8 through October 1) and 82 in Webster Groves (January 5 through September 29). These are past sales, not what’s for sale today.

Is Kirkwood more expensive than Webster Groves?

By the median, yes. By the square foot, barely. Kirkwood’s 2026 median was $566,750 and Webster Groves’ $444,500, a $122,250 gap. But the median Kirkwood house was larger (2,315 vs. 1,936 sq ft) and newer (built 1951 vs. 1936), and price per square foot was nearly even: $268 versus $262.

What does $400,000 buy in Kirkwood vs. Webster Groves?

More chances in Webster Groves. At $400,000 or less, a 2026 budget reached 32 of 82 Webster Groves sales (39.0%) and 21 of 80 Kirkwood sales (26.2%). The houses were similar in both: a median of about 1,250 sq ft, built in the late 1940s.

What about $500,000 or $750,000?

At $500,000 or less: 50 of 82 Webster Groves sales (61.0%) and 35 of 80 in Kirkwood (43.8%). At $750,000 or less: 69 (84.1%) and 55 (68.8%). A $500,000 budget reached most of Webster Groves’ 2026 market and less than half of Kirkwood’s.

Is the same-size house more expensive in Kirkwood?

Not consistently. Each city came out ahead in some size ranges. Homes of 1,600–1,999 sq ft sold for a median $254 a square foot in Kirkwood and $221 in Webster Groves, but homes of 1,200–1,599 sq ft sold for $250 and $275. From 2,000 to 2,499 sq ft they were nearly even, $238 and $242.

Which has older homes, Kirkwood or Webster Groves?

Webster Groves. 45 of its 82 sales in 2026 (54.9%) were built before 1940, versus 17 of 80 (21.2%) in Kirkwood. Kirkwood also had new construction: 10 sales built in 2010 or later, at a median $1,250,000. Webster Groves had none.

Do homes sell faster in Kirkwood or Webster Groves?

About equally fast. The median days on market (CDOM) was 6 in Kirkwood and 5 in Webster Groves, and most sold above the original list price in both: 45 of 80 in Kirkwood (56.2%) and 51 of 82 in Webster Groves (62.2%).

More Kirkwood vs. Webster Groves Questions

Are property taxes higher in Kirkwood or Webster Groves?

A little higher in Kirkwood in dollars, lower as a share of price. The median annual property tax on 2026 sales was $4,995 in Kirkwood, about 0.90% of the sale price, and $4,816 in Webster Groves, about 1.00%, according to MLS sales records. Same-size homes compared →

What does a four-bedroom house cost in Kirkwood vs. Webster Groves?

In 2026 sales, the median home with four or more bedrooms sold for $775,000 in Kirkwood (38 sales) and $710,000 in Webster Groves (29 sales). The Kirkwood houses were larger, 3,007 vs. 2,588 sq ft.

Do sellers take less than asking in Kirkwood or Webster Groves?

About a third do in both: 28 of 80 Kirkwood sales (35.0%) and 27 of 82 in Webster Groves (32.9%) closed below the original list price. When Webster Groves homes sold under, the cut ran deeper, a median 7.8% below the original list price versus 5.3% in Kirkwood.
