What is considered West County St. Louis?

West County isn’t a city or a ZIP code. It’s the common name for the western suburbs of St. Louis County. This guide covers 14 cities: Chesterfield, Wildwood, Ballwin, Ellisville, Manchester, Eureka, Valley Park, Clarkson Valley, Creve Coeur, Des Peres, Town and Country, Frontenac, Ladue and Kirkwood. Some people also include Webster Groves, Maryland Heights or Olivette. Compare all 14 →

How much does a house cost in West County?

It depends heavily on the city. In 2026 closed sales, medians ran from $304,000 in Valley Park to $1,430,000 in Ladue. Eight of the 14 had medians between $416,500 (Manchester) and $685,000 (Chesterfield). See the comparison table →

What are the most affordable cities in West County?

By 2026 median sale price: Valley Park ($304,000, 24 sales), Manchester ($416,500), Ballwin ($419,950) and Ellisville ($440,000, 24 sales). Valley Park homes were the smallest, with a median of 1,451.5 sq ft. See the full comparison →

Can I buy a house in West County for $400,000?

Yes, in some cities. At $400,000 or less, a 2026 budget reached 22 of 24 in Valley Park, 27 of 60 in Manchester, 37 of 86 in Ballwin, 7 of 24 in Ellisville, 15 of 52 in Eureka, 21 of 80 in Kirkwood, 2 of 11 in Frontenac, 3 of 25 in Des Peres, 13 of 136 in Wildwood, 5 of 59 in Creve Coeur and 3 of 120 in Chesterfield. It reached none of the sales in Clarkson Valley, Town and Country or Ladue. See every budget by city →

What does $600,000 buy in West County?

At $600,000 or less, a 2026 budget reached every Valley Park sale and most sales in Ellisville (21 of 24), Manchester (50 of 60), Ballwin (78 of 86), Eureka (40 of 52), Wildwood (72 of 136) and Kirkwood (43 of 80). It reached fewer than half in Creve Coeur (21 of 59), Chesterfield (42 of 120) and Des Peres (7 of 25), 1 or 2 sales each in Clarkson Valley, Town and Country and Frontenac, and none in Ladue. How fast do homes sell in West County?

Fast in most cities. Median days on market ran from 4.5 days in Manchester to 14 days in Ellisville and Town and Country in 2026, with Frontenac at 3 days on 11 sales. Eureka was the exception at 33.5 days. See days on market by city →

Do West County homes sell over asking price?

In six of the 14 cities. Measured against the original list price, more than half of 2026 sales closed above it in Manchester (41 of 60), Frontenac (8 of 11), Des Peres (16 of 25), Kirkwood (45 of 80), Ballwin (48 of 86) and Ladue (14 of 27). Clarkson Valley (4 of 13), Ellisville (8 of 24), Town and Country (11 of 31) and Eureka (19 of 51) had the lowest shares. See sales above list by city → How much should you offer? →

Which school districts serve West County?

Five: Rockwood R-VI, Parkway, Kirkwood R-VII, Ladue and Valley Park. Several cities are split. 2026 sales in Chesterfield were 75 Parkway and 45 Rockwood, Ballwin 53 Rockwood and 33 Parkway, Creve Coeur 45 Parkway and 14 Ladue, and Des Peres 17 Kirkwood and 8 Parkway. Check the district for each address. See the district split by city →

How much are property taxes in West County?

It depends on the school district. The 2026 median annual tax on homes that sold was $3,794 in Valley Park, $4,763 in Parkway, $5,491 in Kirkwood R-VII, $5,793 in Rockwood R-VI and $10,055 in Ladue. Listing taxes are the seller’s most recent bill; confirm the exact address with the St. Louis County Collector of Revenue. See taxes by city and district →

How much does a ranch house cost in West County?

Among cities with at least 8 ranch sales in 2026, median ranch prices ran from $249,500 in Valley Park (8 sales) to $880,000 in Town and Country (9). Ballwin had the most ranch sales (44, median $392,500), followed by Chesterfield and Wildwood (28 each, medians $565,000 and $537,500). See ranch and bedroom prices by city →

How much does a 3- or 4-bedroom house cost in West County?

For a 3-bedroom, 2026 medians ran from $313,000 in Valley Park (15 sales) to $600,000 in Creve Coeur (17) among cities with at least 10 sales. For a 4-bedroom, they ran from $450,000 in Ellisville (10 sales) to $1,360,000 in Ladue (10). Chesterfield had the most 4-bedroom sales: 76, at a median of $655,000. See prices by bedroom count →

How old are most homes in West County?

It varies by city. The median year built was 1951 in Kirkwood and 1955 in Ladue, 1968 in Ballwin and Creve Coeur, 1982 in Chesterfield and Town and Country, 1992 in Wildwood, 1993 in Valley Park and 1999 in Eureka. See the typical house by city →

What Is Each West County City Like?

West County isn’t one market. Here’s each city with its 2026 numbers, grouped by the road that ties it together.

Along Manchester Road and Clarkson Road: Ballwin, Ellisville, Wildwood, Clarkson Valley, Chesterfield

Ballwin (86 sales, median $419,950) had more ranch sales than any other West County city, 44 of 86, and homes with a median build year of 1968. 2026 sales split 53 Rockwood R-VI and 33 Parkway, so check the district for each address.

Ellisville (24 sales, median $440,000) sits west of Ballwin on Manchester Road, all in Rockwood R-VI. It’s a small sample: 7 of the 24 sales closed at $400,000 or less, and the median sale took 14 days.

Wildwood (136 sales, median $599,000) had the most sales in West County. Homes were large (median 3,342 sq ft) and newer (median build year 1992), all in Rockwood R-VI. It’s the farthest-out city on this list, so drive your real commute at rush hour before you commit to an address.

Clarkson Valley (13 sales, median $975,000) is a small city of large homes, a median of 4,959 sq ft. None of its 13 sales closed under $500,000.

Chesterfield (120 sales, median $685,000) is spread out, from older subdivisions near Highway 40 to newer ones farther west. 2026 sales split 75 Parkway and 45 Rockwood R-VI. A 63017 or 63005 mailing address doesn’t mean the house is inside the City of Chesterfield.

Along I-44 and Manchester Road: Eureka, Valley Park, Manchester, Des Peres, Kirkwood

Eureka (52 sales, median $452,000) had the newest homes on this list, a median build year of 1999, and the longest market time, a median of 33.5 days. All 52 sales were in Rockwood R-VI.

Valley Park (24 sales, median $304,000) had the lowest median in West County and the smallest homes, a median of 1,451.5 sq ft. 22 of the 24 sales were in the Valley Park School District.

Manchester (60 sales, median $416,500) sold fast, at a median of 4.5 days, and 41 of 60 sales closed above the original list price. 58 of the 60 sales were in Parkway.

Des Peres (25 sales, median $925,000) had 17 sales in Kirkwood R-VII and 8 in Parkway. Only 3 of the 25 closed at $400,000 or less.

Kirkwood (80 sales, median $566,750) had the oldest homes, a median build year of 1951, and a walkable downtown around the train station. Prices spread widely: the middle half of sales ran from $388,750 to $807,500. All 80 sales were in Kirkwood R-VII.

Along Highway 40 and Olive: Town and Country, Creve Coeur, Frontenac, Ladue

Creve Coeur (59 sales, median $640,000) had 27 ranch sales, close behind Chesterfield and Wildwood (28 each), at a median of $606,500. 2026 sales split 45 Parkway and 14 Ladue.

Town and Country (31 sales, median $1,348,000) had the largest homes after Clarkson Valley, a median of 4,534.5 sq ft. All 31 sales were in Parkway, and none closed under $500,000.

Frontenac (11 sales, median $1,325,000) is a small sample. 8 of the 11 sales closed above the original list price, and the median sale took 3 days.

Ladue (27 sales, median $1,430,000) had the highest median in West County, and its homes were older than most, with a median build year of 1955. All 27 sales were in the Ladue School District, where the 2026 median property tax was $10,055.

Mistakes I See West County Buyers Make

Assuming the city name tells you the school district

It doesn’t. In 2026, Chesterfield, Ballwin, Creve Coeur, Des Peres, Manchester and Valley Park sales all crossed district lines. Check the district for the exact address before a school-driven decision.

Shopping West County like one market

In 2026, $450,000 reached 53 of 86 sales in Ballwin, 37 of 60 in Manchester and 9 of 120 in Chesterfield. Shop by the city and the house, not the West County label.

Skipping the rush-hour drive

Some West County drives look easy on a map until you do them at 7:30 a.m. Drive your real commute at rush hour before you commit to an outer suburb.

Comparing price without comparing taxes

Two houses at the same price can carry very different tax bills depending on the district. 2026 medians ran from $3,794 in Valley Park to $10,055 in Ladue. Put the tax into your monthly payment before you compare.

About the area

Is Kirkwood part of West County?

Usually. Kirkwood sits on the eastern edge of the area most people call West County, and it’s often grouped with Webster Groves and the inner-ring suburbs. This guide includes it because buyers searching West County often compare it with Des Peres and Manchester. Kirkwood guide →

Is West County St. Louis expensive?

It has the widest price range in St. Louis County. 2026 medians ran from $304,000 in Valley Park to $1,430,000 in Ladue. Five cities had medians over $900,000: Des Peres, Clarkson Valley, Frontenac, Town and Country and Ladue. See every city →

What highways serve West County?

Highway 40 (I-64) runs east-west through Chesterfield and Town and Country. I-270 runs north-south past Creve Coeur, Des Peres and Kirkwood. I-44 runs through Valley Park and Eureka. Manchester Road (Route 100) ties together Des Peres, Manchester, Ballwin, Ellisville and Wildwood, and Route 141 runs north-south through the middle.
