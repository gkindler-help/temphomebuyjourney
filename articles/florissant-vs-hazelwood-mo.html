Florissant vs. Hazelwood: What 2026 Sales Show

Last updated October 22, 2026

Florissant cost less in 2026, a $180,000 median versus $214,450 in Hazelwood, but mostly because its houses were smaller and older. Per square foot the two cities sold almost even, $136 and $132. Florissant had more than twice as many sales, which means more chances at a lower budget; Hazelwood’s homes sold faster.

These numbers count only sales inside each city’s limits. Hazelwood is also the name of a school district that covers much of Florissant, so a “Hazelwood” listing may mean the district, not the city. Check the exact address.

Florissant vs. Hazelwood Questions, Answered With 2026 Sales

Short answers first, from 2026 closed single-family sales inside each city’s Census boundary: 167 in Florissant (January 6 through October 1) and 63 in Hazelwood (January 6 through September 30). These are past sales, not what’s for sale today.

Is Hazelwood more expensive than Florissant?

By the median, yes. By the square foot, no. Hazelwood’s 2026 median was $214,450 and Florissant’s $180,000. But the median Hazelwood house was larger (1,566 vs. 1,301 sq ft) and newer (built 1965 vs. 1958), and price per square foot was nearly even: $132 in Hazelwood and $136 in Florissant.

What does $200,000 buy in Florissant vs. Hazelwood?

More chances in Florissant. At $200,000 or less, a 2026 budget reached 107 of 167 Florissant sales (64.1%) and 25 of 63 Hazelwood sales (39.7%). The homes were alike at that budget: a median of about 1,100 sq ft, built in the late 1950s.

What about $150,000 or $250,000?

At $150,000 or less: 46 of 167 Florissant sales (27.5%) and 11 of 63 in Hazelwood (17.5%). At $250,000 or less: 150 (89.8%) and 43 (68.3%). Above $250,000, Hazelwood had 20 sales and Florissant 17.

Is the same-size house more expensive in Hazelwood?

Slightly, from about 1,000 square feet up. Homes of 1,200–1,399 sq ft sold for a median $156 a square foot in Hazelwood and $131 in Florissant, but at 1,400–1,699 sq ft they were nearly even, $132 and $130. Many of these groups are small, so read them as a guide.

Do homes sell faster in Hazelwood or Florissant?

Hazelwood, by a lot. The median days on market (CDOM) was 9 in Hazelwood and 27 in Florissant, and 78 of 167 Florissant sales (46.7%) took 30 days or more, versus 16 of 63 in Hazelwood (25.4%). 29 Hazelwood sales (46.0%) closed above the original list price, versus 57 in Florissant (34.1%).

Which has older homes, Florissant or Hazelwood?

Florissant. 149 of its 167 sales in 2026 (89.2%) were built from 1950 to 1969. Hazelwood had more 1970s and 1980s homes: 13 of 63 (20.6%) were built from 1970 to 1989, versus 4 in Florissant.

More Florissant vs. Hazelwood Questions

Are property taxes higher in Florissant or Hazelwood?

About the same. The median annual property tax on 2026 sales with a tax record was $2,421 in Florissant (about 1.32% of the sale price) and $2,605 in Hazelwood (about 1.26%), according to MLS sales records. In both cities it depends on the school district side. Florissant taxes by district → · Hazelwood taxes by district →

What does a three-bedroom house cost in Florissant vs. Hazelwood?

In 2026 sales, the median three-bedroom home sold for $177,500 in Florissant (121 sales) and $214,000 in Hazelwood (39 sales). The Hazelwood houses were larger, 1,505 vs. 1,278 sq ft.

What does a four-bedroom house cost in Florissant vs. Hazelwood?

In 2026 sales, the median home with four or more bedrooms sold for $243,000 in Florissant (27 sales) and $260,000 in Hazelwood (19 sales), at 1,737 and 1,890 sq ft.
